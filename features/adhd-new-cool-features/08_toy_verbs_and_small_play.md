# Toy verbs and small play

What a curious kid would actually do in this city: self-invented games that
need no quest to exist, and that quietly exercise every serious system.

### Spoken Treasure Maps `[N9 V7 F8]`

Stash an item in one of the hundred small courts, then give an NPC spoken
directions — "left past the Bellstand, under the third arch" — and follow
secretly to see if an LLM can actually navigate your terrible directions. If
she finds it, it's hers and she remembers you as the treasure child; if she
gets lost, watching her wander is even funnier. Player-authored quests with
zero quest system.

### The Personal Prophet `[N8 V8 F8]`

Pick one person and shadow them for a day until you know their whole life —
routes, meals, bedtime, the literal poop clock — then predict it out loud to
their face: "you'll want the privy in ten minutes." The gaze reflex means
they gradually notice the small shadow and start reacting, and by their Night
Office you might be the thing they reflect on. Being someone's personal
prophet is the best kind of creepy.

### Question Cards `[N8 V8 F8]`

Ask every person in the city the same weird question — "what is the moon made
of?" — and collect the answers like cards in a little album screen: a
sergeant, a baker, a gate keeper and a prisoner give genuinely different
LLM answers shaped by who they are. Completing a ward feels like completing a
sticker page. The rarest card is whoever refuses to answer at all.

### The Nickname Book `[N8 V8 F8]`

Strangers show only ids until you know them — which is exactly backwards for
a kid — so name them yourself off one detail (Cabbage-Hat, Fast Grandma, the
Sneezer) in a little book, and the HUD shows *your* names. The gasp moment is
confessing a nickname to its owner: an LLM actor deciding live whether to be
delighted or furious, and maybe the nickname spreading to the ward. You knew
this city before it knew you.

### The Message-Runner `[N7 V8 F9]`

Carry whispered word between NPCs for a crumb of bread: Ilse tells you a
thing, you sprint the doglegs to Odo, and because these people actually
remember and reply, Odo says something *back* — now you're in the middle of a
real argument between two grown-ups, charging one crust per message through
the offer system. A job the player invented that no adult gave them.

### The Planted Lie `[N8 V7 F8]`

Tell one gossipy NPC you're secretly a prince and wait to hear it come back
wrong: the cast remembers, talks, and reflects nightly, so the lie travels
and mutates — a week later a gate keeper bows, or the sergeant asks pointed
questions about your kingdom. An experiment run on the whole city's brain,
and your own lie walks back to you wearing different clothes.

### Race Your Stick `[N7 V9 F7]`

Drop a stick in the canal and race it to the next bridge on foot: it drifts
the straight way while you sprint the doglegs, so the city layout *is* the
opponent. Name the stick, and if it beats you three times it retires into
your pocket as a champion. Zero LLM calls, pure legs and water.

### Crumbs off the Bridges `[N7 V8 F7]`

Drop crumbs and pebbles from the overhead bridges onto people's hats, then
duck: the witness system already notices things within a few meters, so a
bonked NPC looks *up*, and you flatten behind the parapet. If the sergeant
beat passes below it becomes a crime with real ward-notice consequences. The
duck is the whole game.

### The Echo Hunt `[N7 V8 F7]`

Shout in every covered passage to find the one with the best echo, ranked in
your head like a connoisseur — each passage gets an acoustic signature from
the soundscape system. Speech reaches NPCs anywhere, so yelling at midnight
makes shutters bang open and real angry people tell you off by name; the
telling-off is the trophy. The best echo spot becomes *your* spot.

### Trade a Pebble Up `[N8 V6 F7]` ⚠ trap

Offer a worthless pebble to a bored stall keeper, get talked into a turnip,
trade the turnip for a candle, and keep climbing for days until the pebble is
a goose — each LLM merchant genuinely deciding whether your sob story is
worth it. **Trap because:** an economy balanced on LLM merchants being
sweet-talked is an economy speedrun to zero; needs hard value guards before
it's anything but a demo.
