# Reading bodies and windows

Stealth, leverage and negotiation built from systems already paid for: body
clocks, gaze, drunkenness, weather, patrol beats. Mastery is reading a body
or a sky, not a meter.

### The Nineteen-Metre Haggle `[N9 V8 F8]`

Offers already lapse at 20 m — so walking a bargain to the very edge of lapse
becomes a formal negotiation mechanic. Stall-holders visibly sweeten a
standing offer as you drift, follow you three reluctant steps, or let it die
with a shouted last price; distance becomes the haggling meter, readable in
world-space with no UI. A technical timeout converted into body-language
bargaining.

### The Gaze Window `[N7 V8 F8]`

Pockets can be picked only outside an NPC's gaze reflex, and their body
statuses set the window's width: buying a mark two ales or waiting for their
bladder clock literally widens your opening. Mastery is reading a body — the
sway, the fidget, the head-turn cadence, all already animated. Every failure
creates a witness with a real memory instead of a meter penalty.

### Biology Sniping `[N8 V8 F8]`

The poop clock and bladder urgency run on officers too: time your move to a
gate keeper's urgency spike, or induce it by buying him the ale that sets his
clock. Small tells — shifting weight, a glance down the alley — let an
observant player read a guard's body like a patrol pattern. A stealth system
built entirely from mechanics the game already pays for.

### Muddying the Record `[N8 V7 F8]`

Ward notices are written from witness accounts — so getting the sole witness
drunk before the sergeant arrives corrupts what the notice says about you:
wrong coat, wrong square, sometimes wrong culprit entirely. Drunkenness is
already a status the LLM sees, and notices are already the law system's
memory. Evidence-tampering via ale purchases, from three systems that already
talk to each other.

### Rain Windows `[N7 V8 F8]`

Hard rain empties the squares and packs five NPCs shoulder-to-shoulder under
one awning, where speech carries and eavesdropping is unavoidable. Rain
becomes privacy for deeds in the open and forced intimacy under cover — you
check the sky the way a stealth player checks light meters — and it muffles
footsteps, douses lanterns, and shortens every gaze window. Seasoned players
do their worst work wet.

### The Curfew Gauntlet `[N7 V9 F9]`

Being outdoors after the Snuffing becomes a stealth loop played against the
sergeants' real patrol beats: you learn Odo's loop the way a speedrunner
learns a boss pattern, and getting home from a tavern becomes a nightly
tension curve through doglegs and overhead bridges — pure route knowledge,
zero LLM calls. Getting caught feeds the existing gaol-fee economy instead of
a fail state.

### Curfew% `[N8 V8 F8]`

A sanctioned ghost-run discipline: one fence NPC pays for proven after-bell
crossings — "from the Bellstand to the water gate, after the Scold, unseen" —
verified by which bells rang over you en route. The players' own emergent
challenge becomes an in-fiction profession, and getting seized mid-run
doesn't fail the game; it feeds the gaol economy.
