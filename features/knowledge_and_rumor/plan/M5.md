# M5 — Consequence, legibility and the tune

The last milestone. It makes holding a fact matter on a turn nobody mentions it, finishes the mint
whitelist, puts the wave where the player can see it, re-measures the band with all of that live, and
lands the feature.

Read `00_decisions.md`, `01_api.md`, `02_numbers.md` and `03_assets.md` first. Every signature and
every number below is theirs; this file adds bodies, call sites and tests. Decisions are cited as
`(D-n)`; anchors as `path:line (marker)`. **Every anchor in this file was re-opened and re-verified
against `develop @ f56a2c3` while it was written** — line numbers drift, the markers do not. Where a
prior draft's anchor was wrong the correction is marked `> CORRECTION:` in place, so you can trust the
rest.

Two rules bind the whole milestone:

- **Step 16 (the tune) runs last and alone** (D45). It re-measures against M2's checked-in baseline,
  so nothing else may be in flight when it does.
- **No new probability.** The spec names none in M5, and every reading below is a deterministic
  predicate or a `max`. Where a number was unavoidable it is an existing constant reused, and the
  reuse is justified where it happens. Grep your own diff for `hash`, `roll`, `rand` and `_CHANCE`
  before you commit: a new draw in M5 invalidates M2's cadence measurement and no test will notice.

        &mut self.world,
        at,
        crate::knowledge::KNELL_CARRY_M,

```rust
/// A life counted out of Saint Maren's. `Blood`, the top band. It garbles the
/// **day** and nothing else: the place is one tower and everybody knows which,
/// and `subject` is **empty** — nobody is named, which is the whole point of

/// to swap and the row's mask says so (a departure from the spec's "subject,
/// day", recorded at close-out; a mask naming a field the sentence cannot show
/// fails `every_mint_template_names_what_it_garbles`). `quiet_among` is empty
/// and `craft_ear` is `None`.
///
/// The `said` template lives on the `MINT_KINDS` row like every coded mint's
/// (M2 step 16): `{years}` is substituted here, `{day}` by `render_line`.
///
/// The id is `knell.<game day>.<years>`, so two deaths of the same age on the
/// same game day are one fact. That collapse is deliberate and it is the correct
/// reading: the ward is saying *a life of seventeen years was counted out*, and
/// it says it once. An id keyed on a counter instead would be unstable across
/// runs and no test could pin it.
pub fn mint_knell(world: &mut World, at: Vec3, years: u32, game_days: f64) -> Option<FactKey> {
    if !world.knowledge_enabled {
        return None;
    }
    let row = MINT_KINDS.iter().find(|row| row.kind == "knell")?;
    let day = game_days.floor() as i64;
    mint(
        world,
        FactId::from_raw(format!("knell.{day}.{years}")),
        row.topic,
        row.said.replace("{years}", &years.to_string()),
        Vec::new(),
        at,
        row.garble,
        true,                                       // news, and news cools
        FactSource::event("knell", world.event_sequence),
        Some(game_days),
    )
}
```

(`event.rs:113`) requires a non-optional `actor_id`, and `blank` is module-private). `source` is never
rendered, so the sequence in it is provenance only.

#### 3. `pollen::amplify` — a peal re-heats, and never mints

**`crates/cathedral-sim/src/knowledge/pollen.rs`.** The signature is fixed by `01_api.md`:

Body, in order:

2. **Which wards are in earshot.** Walk `ward_grid()`'s cells and collect every ward whose cell

`heat_pct` **before** the change test is the graft: one cooling step multiplies heat by ~0.9439,
twelve orders of magnitude above `f32::EPSILON`, so a raw `!=` is true on every sweep and churns
forever (`marks::published_strength_pct`, `marks.rs:710`, is the precedent).

**Why `REHEAT_TO` and not a louder number.** The spec gives a peal no heat. `REHEAT_TO` is *"where a
re-asked cold fact comes back to, and no further"* (`02_numbers.md` §3), and a bell is the city asking
about a thing out loud — the same case, one rung above nothing. Reusing it means M5 adds no number,
and it keeps the derived property intact: at `0.1309` a re-heated `Blood` row volunteers again
(`0.1309 × 1.00 > 0.119`) and a re-heated off-trade `Craft` row does not
(`0.1309 × 0.12 = 0.0157 < 0.119`). Do **not** re-heat to 1.0: a bell would then make every stale
death as loud as a fresh one and the top band's 36.9 gh news life would become unbounded. Implement
the per-row write as `Knowledge::stir_up(ward, key, REHEAT_TO, game_days)` (M2, D28) — the one
re-heat path — rather than a second `get_mut` loop, so a peal and a re-ask cannot come to differ.

`amplify` writes no percept, publishes nothing, and calls no `touch_public_state` (D12).

## Notes for the next milestone

There is no next milestone. What M5 leaves is a set of seams, one open arm, and four named children.

**The seam the quests take.** Three things, and nothing else:

1. `FactCatalog::extend_from_json(&mut self, json) -> Result<usize, FactCatalogError>` — a quest pack.
   A duplicate id is an error, never a silent overwrite.
2. `World::arm_actor(&mut self, id, goal: Option<String>)` — the goal, and **only** the goal (D37).
   Anything quest-critical is a one-person fact with an `own` string instead: un-`forget`-able,
   re-derived every turn, invalidatable by the sim.
3. `EngineMessage::Journal { entries, standing }` — the quest supplies the two standing lines and the
   journal renders them. The journal knows nothing about quests and must not learn.

**The one arm left open.** `Provenance::QuestPhase { quest, phase }` returns `true` from `still_true`
because there is no quest host to ask. When one lands, that arm is where it binds — one lookup against
the host's live phase — and until then a quest advancing its own phase must call
`Knowledge::invalidate` itself. That is stated in the arm's comment.

**Four children, all designed, none started** (`02_rumor_pollen.md`, "Children"):

- **Magnitude drift** — a signed step in `FactView` beside `day_offset`, biased **upward** in
  proportion to salience. Deliberately not shipped: it needs a fact to carry a number and most do
  not, so it buys embellishment for a minority of the store where hedge erosion bought it for all of
  it. `FactView` has room; `GarbleMask` would need a fourth bool.
- **Sincerity** — `raise_word` is neutral about whether the speaker believes themselves. A private
  flag on `Provenance::Claimed` that the sim reads (they do not repeat it; they deny it if asked
  twice) is *"`holds()` at a decision the sim already makes"* — the same shape as M5's four readings,
  and the sealed payload is already the right place for it, precisely because nothing renders it.
- **Night Office settlement** — include the ward's hottest air in the curfew batch prompt (`night.rs`)
  so it shapes the returned Minor mood, and let a Major's reflection settle a rumour into permanent
  memory. Pollen graduating into canon, on prompts already paid for. `Knowledge::ward_air` is the read
  it needs and it exists.
- **The STRANGER token's other half** — M5 mints on two deed kinds. `STRANGER_DEED_KINDS` is a
  one-line list and the honest way to widen it, one kind at a time, each with a `said` template and a
  stated reason. Do not replace it with a classifier.

**A fifth, now that the bells have a seam.** `EngineCommand::CivicPeal` gives
`features/false_peals__ring_the_bells_manually.md` its epistemic consequence for free: a player who
rings the Scold out of hour re-heats the ward's `Law` air, and the ward starts talking about the law
again with nothing having happened. That is the false-bell prank's payoff and it needs no new code —
only a way for the player to pull the rope.

**Three things a later change will be tempted to break, and all three have tests.**

- **The self-subject rule** (T3, T9). The player is the commonest subject in the game once the
  STRANGER token is live, and giving them a deposit path for facts about themselves looks like a fix
  and is not; the payoff is the greeting register and their own mouth, not a receipt.
- **`Rung::Hearsay`'s declaration position** (T7). `Ord` here is declaration order, and moving that
  variant for tidiness sorts a hearsay word above a warrant on the HUD's standing line.
- **`raise_hearsay_words` calling `Notices::raise_hearsay` directly** (T8). Routing it through
  `actions::raise_notice` for symmetry re-enters M2's mint hook and turns one garbled fact into an
  unbounded chain of them.
