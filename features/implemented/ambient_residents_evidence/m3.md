# M3 — Integration and measured route geometry

The resident controller now releases its old reservations without erasing a
fresh explicit-intent route installed earlier in the poll. Conversations cancel
optional targets immediately, retain a still-occupied origin, hold the actor,
and resume with a dwell or one committed return. Settled conversations remain
eligible for local meal support across two normal-length days. Recovery can
reserve its own retained origin (including cancellation/departure while that
origin is also the destination), so a full patch cannot strand a displaced body.

Necessary returns use `NavData::connected_route`: bounded exact local connectors
at both ends, measured graph lanes between them, and complete segment validation.
Only the graph search is city-wide. Optional walks still use M2's 15 m local
budget; ordinary local domestic connections use 30 m. A failed journey backs
off in place. The integration regression actually walks an explicit visit more
than 60 m away, reaches it, returns once and remains settled for another 30 s,
checking each displacement against walking speed and exact swept clearance.

Custody, escort duties, departure, absence, removal, release, curfew and lightning
retain ownership priority. Cleanup of a removed nonmoving patchmate cannot
release another resident's optional departure flag. Fresh explicit directions
retain the existing ability to leave an exchange; a conversation that stops an
ongoing intent holds its resumption. Curfew can end that intent during the hold.
The optional cap excludes held, absent, retiring, departing, escorting, explicit,
lightning-paused and resident-sheltering actors. It is enforced after admission
as well, since acquiring shelter can reduce eligibility in the same poll.

## Local weather shelter

The resident baker adds **159 separate safe standing positions under 10 actual
authored roof polygons**, with the same physical/door/through-route exclusions
and global 1.6 m spacing as frontage spots. They are reservation destinations,
not public places. Residents index the roofs within 30 m of their familiar
patch once at enrollment; acquisition then requires a bounded 30 m exact route,
a free individual claim, physical vacancy and roof capacity. Optional movement
admission never authorizes emergency traffic.

The ordinary global shelter ladder remains for authored actors. Residents use
local claims and the existing weather thresholds/release hysteresis. An already
settled shelter conversation keeps its place. Leaving cover, interruption,
closure, custody and removal release claims appropriately. Roof exposure comes
from the actual shelter polygon at the body's actual position. A house door or
home assignment never manufactures shelter or an indoors state.

Capacity is intentionally limited: a narrow covered passage may have no safe
place to stop, and a resident with no available local cover waits truthfully
exposed. The requirement does not justify sending the whole city to one distant
awning. M4 should include rain views alongside its calm-weather measurements.

## Snapshot, prompt and actor sheet

`CharacterState::resident` carries the projected `ResidentStatus`; it crosses
both the simulation snapshot and the Bevy world mirror. Status includes the
actual occupied/destination claim, familiar patch/description, phase, remaining
dwell, movement cause, actual roof exposure and separate outdoor resting proxies.
`WaitingToReturn` describes a failed/displaced wait without pretending it is
rest at the household. Phase/claim changes publish a revision even without
movement; counting down the dwell does not force a full snapshot every frame.
The projection updates numeric fields without reallocating unchanged strings.

Prompts use natural local-routine prose and an unnumbered `your_routine`, rather
than pretending the resident has editable occupational legs. Private spot IDs
remain absent from the prompt and public wayfinding. The debug actor sheet
shows the handles, dwell and cause and names local changes, returns, weather
and custody correctly. The full present-population denominator remains intact;
indoors is still unavailable. The trace header now acknowledges resident patches.

## Geometry and reproducibility

`scripts/bake_route_clearance.py` performs append-only localized repairs and
subdivision. All **3,972 original nodes** retain their exact coordinates and
indices, preserving `places.json`, `shelters.json`, named places, sites and doors.
`navigation.json:endpoint_nodes` preserves original nearest-node snapping for
authored destinations; subdivision vertices provide lane geometry and exact
connectors without silently choosing a neighbouring alley as the destination.

Widths use distance to blocked cells of the already body-eroded surface,
subtracting the blocked-cell half diagonal, sample-to-cell-centre offset and
half the sampling gap. A pinch therefore keeps its narrow width; it no longer
collapses a long open stretch to 0.6 m. Runtime lanes validate their shifts and
all connections by exact supercover, backing off safely. An invalid legacy
centreline cannot become the fallback route. Actual authored and resident steps
and separation pushes use the same swept validation, including the connector
to the next waypoint. Needle detours and off-graph intent, lamp and food-pitch
connections are checked too. No speed, job, schedule or named destination changed.

The independent [route proof](m3_route_validation.json) rebuilds from the original
graph pinned by `baseline_meta.json:head`, so a later commit cannot silently
change its baseline. It requires object equality with final geometry, refines the result again
to prove idempotence, and checks external references. Rounding tolerance prevents
a nominal 4 m subdivision from splitting again on a floating-point tail.
The report path is optional, not tied to the feature's eventual directory.

Final resident rebake: **2,682 patches / 9,232 frontage spots**, including 7,105
home-connected spots and the same 939 usable doors. Independent minimum clearances:
1.6008 m between centres, 2.1213 m from a door, 1.5038 m from a through-route
centreline and 0.5637 m from physical collision geometry. The full metadata
