Status: M1 complete (2026-09-06). Default generated behaviour is unchanged; M2–M4 remain required.

# M1 — Places and paths

The navigation asset now includes optional `resident_places` metadata. Existing
nodes, edges, widths, places, sites, doors and bitset remain identical to HEAD;
only the new metadata differs. A read-only parsed-document comparison proved
this. Small existing test graphs omit the field and receive an empty catalogue.
No resident is enrolled by M1. GPU work remains with the parent/M4.

## Geometry and capacity

The final deterministic bake provides **2,665 patches and 9,171 individual
spots**, with 2–5 spots per patch (schema permits 2–6). These are building-edge
frontages, including frontages beside courts and squares, not landmark-centre
nodes. Candidate steps are 2 m, grouped into 10 m frontage chunks; accepted
patches normally span 2–8 m. Stable identifiers encode building, edge, chunk,
and candidate ordinal. Facing hints look outward from the frontage. Descriptions
use building names or their cadastral district; M2 must use the existing ward
resolver for spoken mechanical wards, since cadastral districts include combined
labels such as “Bell and Sluice Wards”.

The bake rejects isolated/singleton groups, blocked local connections, door
approaches, graph through corridors (including doglegs), the Gradine stairs,
bridge footprints, named bridge/gate/Needle areas, and service areas around
water sources and food/counter pitches. Food offsets and their runtime fallback
site are both protected; anchored counters use their current home door. All
candidates also require extra margin inside the eroded walkable component and
an **exact collision-footprint check**. Global spacing is checked across patch
and building boundaries, not merely within a patch.

| Clearance | Required | Final measured minimum |
|---|---:|---:|
| Between standing centres | 1.6 m | 1.6008 m |
| Door node to standing centre | 2.0 m | 2.1213 m |
| Through-route centreline to standing centre | 1.3 m | 1.4798 m |
| Physical collider to standing centre | 0.55 m | 0.5637 m |

The 0.55 m physical margin includes a 0.35 m body, 0.15 m arrival tolerance
and 0.05 m spare clearance. The through-route exclusion raster includes a
0.25 m rounding allowance. These are standing-clearance thresholds; no route
half-width has been raised. An independent Euclidean check caught a 1.23 m
slanted-edge raster exclusion and a 0.419 m thin/diagonal-wall standing point
in early bakes; the final bake corrects both rather than weakening the checks.

The four M0 observation boxes have capacity 15 at Wickmarket, 14 at Tenterhook
Lane, 11 at Burnt Court and 1 at the Needle's surrounding box. The Needle
choke itself remains excluded. Membership in an observation box is not patch
membership. Geometry and example stable spot IDs, cadastral distribution, and
the deterministic-rebake result are in [m1_geometry.json](m1_geometry.json).

Requests of 1,000 and 2,000 fit this catalogue without stacking or reduced
clearance. At a 20,000 request, the raw geometry ceiling is 9,171 placed and
10,829 unplaced **before** additional household/dynamic occupancy constraints.
M2 must report its actual requested/placed/unplaced counts and leave excess
actors unspawned. This is a geometry capacity observation, not a performance
promise or a claim that M1 changed the live generator.

## Homes and resting: important M2 boundary

**2,155 patches / 7,093 spots connect to 939 distinct doors.** Each home path
starts at the first spot and ends at the exact door node. The maximum straight
spot-to-anchor distance plus the baked home path is at most **30 m**, so every
spot in that patch has a verified bounded access route. A patch with no valid
nearby same-building door has `home_building: null` and an empty `home_path`.

Per-door caps of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 provide housed daytime capacities of 939,
1,878, 2,739, 3,541 and 4,263 respectively. The existing approximate caps at
1,000/2,000 requests can therefore support their approximately 75% housed
populations **if allocation balances eligible doors**. Do not take the first
N lexically sorted spots and then discover that their few houses are full.
Distribute allocation across patches/doors deterministically and reserve spare
positions for optional movement.

**Home paths are access metadata, not standing permission.** Ending all
residents on their door node would block the entrance and stack bodies. Only
176 current safe spots lie within 3 m of their connected home door. There are
1,631 within 8 m and 3,338 within 10 m (near-home measurements before the final
three-candidate wall filter; use current geometry for allocations). M2 must
reserve suitable safe frontage/rest positions and record its resting state
truthfully; it must not fake an indoors state or manipulate M0's existing
home-distance proxy. There is no hidden-actor/interior representation here.
Safe domestic routing/placement and its routine are M2's work.

## Runtime schema and APIs

All geometry loads through `NavData::from_parts(&str, &[u8])`; the sim remains
IO-free. `NavData::resident_places()` exposes the catalogue in
`nav/residents.rs`:

- `ResidentPatch`: stable `id`, `building`, meaningful `description`, `capacity`,
  `spots`, `graph_node`, `graph_path`, optional `home_building`, `home_path`.
- `StandingSpot`: stable `id`, exact `xz`, `facing_yaw`, `position()` at WALK_Y,
  and `contains(position)` with the 0.15 m XZ arrival tolerance.
- `ResidentPlaces::capacity()`, `spot(id)` and `patch_for_spot(id)` are convenient
  catalogue lookups. **The latter two scan linearly. M2 must index/store patch
  and spot handles at enrollment, rather than call them for every actor poll.**

The loader checks patch/spot ID uniqueness, capacity bounds and consistency,
finite facing, cross-patch spacing, exact intra-patch links, graph endpoint
links, valid door references and the home-distance budget. Global graph
reachability and independent collider/door/service exclusion checks are bake
acceptance, tested against the committed asset. Do not fabricate an internal
spot as an advertised public `Place`.

`NavData::local_route(from, to, LocalPathBudget)` in `nav/local.rs`:

- `CHANGE_SPOT` allows 15 m and 4,096 expanded cells; `VISIT` allows 30 m and
  8,192 cells. Public budgets above 30 m/8,192 expansions are rejected. Search
  is limited spatially around the origin and to 65,536 working cells.
- Prefer a validated direct segment, otherwise eight-neighbour grid A* with
  no diagonal corner cutting. Exact endpoint connectors count in the budget.
  The heuristic prunes paths which cannot fit. Simplification revalidates
  every segment, and the final route length must still fit.
- `segment_walkable_exact` visits every grid crossing and every interval,
  including both cells on boundaries and four cells at corner touches. It
  is conservative over the existing agent-eroded walkable surface; finite
  endpoints alone never establish route safety.
- `LocalRoute` carries exact endpoints, the polyline, `length_m` and
  `expanded_cells`. `into_movement(gait_phase)` drops only the exact start and
  sets `Movement::exact_local = true`. Use this constructor for resident walks;
  do not feed them through the old snapped `route_path_to_point` helper.
- Existing constructors explicitly leave `exact_local = false`. The mover
  validates complete exact-local steps and avoidance pushes, including the
  connector from the displaced position to its next waypoint. An unsafe step
  ends the path in place; a prolonged Needle wait also ends it instead of
  substituting an unbounded graph detour. The routine must distinguish failed
  paths from arrival by checking its reserved spot, release the target and
  dwell/back off. No teleport or unchecked final stride is introduced.

`SpotReservations::new(places)` indexes spot positions once. It owns at most
one current spot and one destination per actor and never scans the population:

1. `occupy(actor, spot, position)` claims a free spot at actual arrival.
2. `reserve_destination(actor, spot)` claims a free target before committing
   movement. It cannot accumulate several future spots; a same-poll competitor
   sees the first reservation and fails safely.
3. `release_departed(actor, position)` retains the origin until the body is
   more than 0.85 m away. It can also release a spot during explicit departure.
4. After movement ends, `settle(actor, position)` converts the destination to
   occupied only within 0.15 m, releasing the old claim. The usual final
   avoidance shove is at most 0.045 m at 20 Hz, so this starts one dwell and
   does not justify a corrective walk.
5. `cancel_destination(actor)` handles failed routes/interruption, retaining a
   still-occupied origin. `release_actor(actor)` is idempotent full cleanup for
   custody, leaving town or removal. M2/M3 must call these at their ownership
   transitions. These hooks are not wired to the resident routine in M1.

`owner`, `claims`, `is_available`, `occupied_count` and `destination_count`
provide diagnostics/admission checks. An unavailable destination is a normal
failure; staying at the current valid spot is successful. Runtime dynamic
obstacles/explicit actors can still occupy a baked candidate, so admission
must retain existing local avoidance and sensible occupancy ownership.
