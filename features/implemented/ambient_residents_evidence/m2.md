# M2 — Residents by default

Both shipped hosts now generate only jobless residents. The approximately
one-quarter hardship draw is retained independently of jobs. The remaining
residents have ordinary household-supported backgrounds without `pauper`.
Default occupation, occupation display, title and rank are null. Civilian
appearance reuses laborer, craftsman and merchant clothes without assigning
those occupations. Authored appearance and occupational policy are retained.

## Generation and capacity

`generate_ambient(nav, count, first_index, occupied, overrides)` returns
`GeneratedCrowd { sheets, placement }`. Both hosts pass their authored spawn
positions as blockers and an empty worker override list. `CrowdPlacement`
reports requested, placed, unplaced, housed, hardship, workers and door cap.
The compatibility `extra_ambient_sheets` wrapper uses the input point count
as its request; graph offsets are no longer spawn permission.

`GeneratedRoutine::Resident { patch, spot }` carries internal baked handles.
`GeneratedRoutine::Worker { occupation, workplace }` requires an explicit
`WorkerOverride`: a registered trade, a compatible named workplace and an
archetype with a work leg. The named workplace is the binding origin, so
another candidate nearer the spawn cannot override it. A non-nearest
cargo-worker workplace regression verifies this. Authored lore constructors
leave `generated_routine: None`.

Housing allocation balances connected doors deterministically, then places
hardship residents in lightly occupied patches. Within a home patch it
prefers safe spots nearest the door. The actor's home sentence, mechanical
home and ward agree. The cap limits generated household assignments; it is
based on the household-eligible request and the 939 connected doors.
Hardship residents have no household door and describe local support/rest.

## Controller and meals

`round/residents.rs` indexes patch/spot metadata once during enrollment and
uses M1's `SpotReservations`. Residents bypass the occupational work, social,
wander/recall, hunger-stall and water rungs. The ambient Night Office reroll
excludes this explicit resident policy while retaining authored ambient and
explicit generated worker behavior.

After settling, deterministic dwells last 45–150 elapsed seconds. At a dwell
deadline a 65% decision may try another spot in the same patch; otherwise a
new dwell starts. Candidates are 1.6–8.1 m away (the catalogue spaces spots
by 1.6 m) and require an exact route within 15 m. M2 optional walks remain
within the actor's own patch. The rotating admission cursor admits at most
floor(eligible / 10) optional walkers, with one slot below ten eligible
residents, and one optional departure per patch. Real bodies are checked only
when proposing a destination, in addition to reservation ownership.

Every walk reserves its destination before commitment. The origin is released
after the body clears it. Actual arrival settles the exact reserved spot;
stopped-short routes release the target and begin a dwell/backoff. Sparse
polling does not accumulate old wander opportunities. A 600-second fixture
through the **full round tick** admitted 198 distinct optional walkers among
200 residents, peaking at 8 concurrent walks, with zero food/water errands or
queues and no occupational legs.

Individual morning/evening retirement choices use 05–07 and 18–20:30 windows.
Retirement prefers the original safe spot; an already occupied safe spot in
the same household frontage is a valid fallback. Domestic returns use the
30 m local route budget. Door paths remain home access metadata, never
permission to stand in an entrance. Night-resting residents remain `InCity`,
visible and interactable. There is no indoors state, hiding or teleport. The
old three-metre home census is retained as a separate overlapping proxy.

Support explicitly requires a settled claimed patch spot, no active travel
intent, and eligibility at both ends of the accounting interval. **Kindling
breakfast (05–07)** supplements the existing 12–21 meal offices at the existing
game-second hearth refill rate. The unchanged hunger decay exhausts a full
gauge in ten game hours; the former 21–12 gap otherwise creates a famished
cohort. Initial needs and missed meals can receive at most one gauge of local
recovery at the same rate. A normal meal closes that recovery allowance.
This changes only need satisfaction: no food, stock, wages or money transfers
are introduced, and real held/offered food and household settlement remain
on their existing code paths.

The needs fixture ran **two 3,600-second days with 0.05-second steps**. After
60 seconds of initial recovery, minimum hunger was **51.000**, above the
famished threshold 15. Inventory/stock/purse state was identical before and
after this isolated support test. A missed-meal return receives bounded local
recovery outside the meal office; a duplicate timestamp gives no new support.
The first run found an exact-07:00 floating-point tail that incorrectly erased
initial recovery. Overlap below 1e-12 days is now zero; regressions cover
several day epochs, ordinary breakfast and complete-day overlap.

## APIs and remaining M3 work

`Round::resident_status(nav, world, id, now)` exposes phase, patch/description,
occupied/destination spot, dwell remaining, optional-walk status, and separate
household-frontage/no-home resting proxies. `resident_count()` and
`resident_reservations()` provide read-only diagnostics. Motion census adds
resident counts, lingering/resting proxies, occupied patches and destination
claims while retaining all `InCity` bodies in the original denominator.
Optional walks currently use diagnostic cause `Wander`; returns use `Domestic`.

Basic conversation interruption, explicit intent/weather handoff, curfew and
custody/absence cleanup exist. M3 still owns exhaustive integration:

- Complete far explicit travel and one safe return after release. Current
  recovery only tries a bounded 30 m path and backs off when farther away.
  Preserve intent ownership when `tick_intents` replaces a resident path
  before the resident tick; cancelling the old reservation must not erase
  the newly owned path.
- Exercise conversation during dwell/motion and immediate target cleanup.
  Current held conversations mark the resident interrupted; meal support
  recovers on release. Audit prolonged conversations during meal offices.
- Prefer local independently reserved weather shelter; audit lightning,
  custody/escort, release, absence and actor removal. Generic weather is the
  present fallback. Cleanup must not clear another resident's departing-patch
  flag when a nonmoving patch mate disappears.
- Handle displacement 0.15–0.85 m from an occupied spot: the origin claim is
  intentionally retained until the body clears 0.85 m. Recovery must be able
  to use its own retained origin when all other patch spots are full.
- Complete prompt/snapshot/debug projection through the status seam. Internal
  spot IDs must not become public wayfinding places. Existing daily-round
  prose already describes local lingering, support and frontage rest.
- Complete shared lane-width/corner repairs from M1 and parent review notes.
  M2 did not rebake or alter navigation geometry.

M4 still owes two-day 1,000/2,000 full-engine measurements, stationary and
