PLACEHOLDER IGNORANCE RULE. The unconditional paragraph goes here, on every
sheet, holder or not — "if you are asked something that is not here and you
were not there, you do not know it: say so plainly, and if you can think who
would (by their trade, their post, or where they were) name them. Never supply
a name, a day, a place or a number you were not given. A guess said aloud
becomes what the ward believes." Nothing has been written yet.
