-------------------------------------------------------------------- R1, hedges.toml

WHAT: 5 rungs x 3 bands (15 keys) -> 7 rungs x 3 bands (21 keys). Unchanged, byte
for byte: hops0_own, hops0, hops1 and cold in all three bands, plus
top_band.hops2 ("They say: %s") and low_band.hops2 (the somebody-who-had-it-from-
somebody line) — those two were already the right rung for two removes in their
own band, and both were measured. NEW: default.hops2, and hops3 + hops4 in all
three bands.

    [default]
    hops2 = "The one who told you was not there either — they had it from the one who was: %s"
    hops3 = "It came to you down a line of mouths, and whoever at the end of it
             actually saw anything is a stranger to you: %s"
    hops4 = "This has been through a great many mouths before it reached yours,
             worn smooth in the telling, and nobody can now say who started it: %s"
    [top_band]
    hops3 = "It is the common word now, and nobody bothers to say where they had it: %s"
    hops4 = "This is simply what is known in this city now, whoever said it first: %s"
    [low_band]
    hops3 = "Passed to you at a remove you cannot account for, the sort of small news
             that gets carried because there is nothing else to carry: %s"
    hops4 = "Stall talk, handed along so often that its edges are gone, and you have
             never met anyone who was there: %s"

WHY: M0's ladder collapsed every telling at two removes or more into one rung, so
of the eight q4_wick holders four (hops 2, 2, 3, 4) received a BYTE-IDENTICAL
knowledge line and two more (hops 1, 1) received another — six of eight holders
got one of two lines. The parroting numbers M0 measured are a floor on both
providers for that reason. Under the new ladder the same eight sheets render SIX
distinct lines instead of four (04 and 05 still share, because both are authored
at hops 2 and the fixtures' hop values were not touched).

Two constraints held from the frozen file:
  * one placeholder per rung, and no count ever reaches the model. {hops} stays
    unused as in v2, and no NEW rung says "second-hand", "third-hand" or
    "fourth-hand" either: an ordinal of removes is the count in words, and it is
    exactly the phrase v3_register got parroted back four times. (default.hops2's
    frozen "Third-hand, mouth to mouth…" is the one wording R1 retires, and this
    is the second reason for retiring it.)
  * the erosion relationship of 02_rumor_pollen.md survives, and is now legible
    across four rungs instead of two. Read the three hops4 rungs against each
    other: "simply what is known in this city now" (top band, hedges shed),
    "nobody can now say who started it" (default), "you have never met anyone who
    was there" (low band, chain named out loud all the way out).

Each rung is a different SHAPE, not the same shape with a stronger adjective —
one names who was and was not there, one names the line the telling came down,
one names what the telling has become. Shape is what has to differ, because the
failure being defeated is a terse model with one sentence of room reciting the
rendered line back.

--------------------------------------------------------------- R2, ignorance_rule

WHAT: two bracketed exemplars replaced by descriptions of the same two moves. The
third bullet, and every other clause of the paragraph, is untouched.

    -  - name the trade that would know ("ask a porter, they stand on that gate");
    -  - name the post whose business it is ("that is the weigher's, not mine");
    +  - name the trade that would know it, whoever handles that sort of thing all day;
    +  - name the post whose business it is, or the officer who keeps such things;
       - name where it happened, or who was standing there.

WHY: the porter exemplar came back in 4 of v2's 22 moonshot replies, once
byte-identical, and openai lifted the weigher one ("Ask the weigher at the Stone
Gate") — confirmed on two providers. A city where every stranger sends you to the
porters is the parroting risk wearing a different coat.

This is the "move with no quotable half-line" option rather than the alternating-
exemplar one, for two reasons. Alternation is not available: the prose is one
static string in strings.toml, so "alternating" would mean two exemplars per
bullet, which is more quotable half-lines and not fewer. And the replacement
clauses are instructions to the reader, not utterances — nobody says "whoever
handles that sort of thing all day" to a stranger in a market — so there is
nothing left in the bullet that can be lifted verbatim into speech. The third
bullet has never carried an exemplar and worked, which is the precedent.

------------------------------------------------------------------ R3, block_note

WHAT: one sentence appended to the end of the paragraph. Nothing else moves — the
clause is appended rather than spliced after "nobody tells anything" precisely so
that every existing line of the note stays byte-identical and the diff is only the
new text.

    + Even so, a thing you hold that bears on what is being asked of you right
    + now, or on what you are about to refuse somebody, is not a thing to sit on:
    + say that one.

WHY, and the reason this is a RE-CHECK and not a repair: the frozen let-it-lie
clause is the prime suspect for M0's single Q1 failure (a butcher refused credit
off a two-year debt memory and never mentioned the arrest that justified the
refusal) — but the same miss came out REPAIRED with not one byte changed when the
paragraph was re-fired at its real shipping position. So the wording may be
innocent, and M0's own note says to re-check before applying. v6_both carries the
FROZEN clause and v7_letitlie the softened one, both at the shipping position, so
the pair is the test. The appended sentence names the exact failure shape rather
than the general one: a fact that explains a refusal you are about to make.
