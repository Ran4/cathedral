   eligible seller after the half-game-day pause; all-refusing cases retain household food fallback.

   expiry, alternative purchase, cooling, invalidation, ablation and cache pruning. Clockless

   independently of the novelty requirement. Inboxes, explicit reactions/priority and All mode
   retain their escape paths; both bodies must be within the resident's own 10 m home radius.

   predicate now excludes hearsay, including after escalation. The first/lowest rung can still
   use the existing summons, settlement and warrant ladder. Direct notice insertion avoids the
   coded notice mint, and stale invalidation precedes the daily reading. Dedupe is bounded by
   `(fact, wrong subject)` and removed with the fact; tests check no event/fact feedback.
4. **Repeated arrivals inflated the journal's separate-mouth count.** The M4 per-stir memo did
   not make A→B→A across different stirs two mouths. Bounded receipt mouth sets now do, with one
   unattributed observation at most, while preserving a closer receipt and avoiding unchanged
   revision bumps. The player's actual mark deeds gain witnessed “You …” receipts without a
   self-subject carrying exception.
5. **Item invalidation and allocation accounting were incomplete.** An ItemWith source now
   requires the item to exist as well as remain in the holder's id list. Source strings are counted
   through a private byte-only accessor. The store also counts vector capacity, household/craft
   data, receipt sets and hearsay memo; consequence caches are separately exposed as a size only.
   The synthetic bound drops earlier timing clones before saturation, preventing Arc COW from
   shrinking capacity eight to six and making the test underreport allocations.
6. **Bell payload and amplifier edge cases needed exact tests.** The engine receives accepted,
   clamped BellPlan strokes once per peal, with the actual tower/carry. Amplification selects
   existing matching air by planar grid-cell coverage plus the own ward, not map centroids.
   Raw heat rises to the target even when the whole percent is unchanged, but that case does not
   increment stir. Generic M2 `stir_up` remains unchanged. Real command tests cover anonymous
   knells, both player mark actions, absent air, outside wards and nonzero-radius cell coverage.
7. **Greeting relevance could seat an unrenderable self-garble.** Both canonical and effective
   self guards now match the final renderer. The already-computed nearby people feed the shared
   seat selection used by rendering and reheating, avoiding another neighbor scan. The old test
   helper's accidental equal-length-id co-location was corrected with explicit proximity fixtures.
8. **Locative and singular prose exposed composition errors.** Sim/host composition fixes doubled
   “at In/Inside/Next to/At” and singular mouth/ward/other/has grammar, without modifying measured
   prompt strings, hedge/ignorance text, area labels or goldens.
