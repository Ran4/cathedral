## Corrections needed to make the milestone's paths meet its decisions

- **The offered occasion keeps its provenance.** A new assertion or event cannot
  replace an offered slot's subject or source while its sheet is in flight. The
  one-slot policy ignores that newer occasion until the exchange finishes. This
  closes the case where A speaks, the sheet renders, B speaks, then A's reply
  incorrectly claims B as its source. Completion, provider failure, stale
  completion and cognition-busy rejection all withdraw the offer and clear the
  relevant seated keys. No queue or new occasion lifetime was added.
- **The cap refusal is reachable.** `raise_word` checks the per-office cap before
  the shared `may_raise_word` guard because that predicate also checks the cap.
  Checking the predicate first would make `WordAlreadySaid` unreachable. Collision
  and other failures spend neither the occasion nor a successful-raise count.
- **A claim's initial air row records its mouth.** The claim installer seats its
  speaker with the occasion's source and gives its initial air row that speaker as
  `via`. Ordinary coded mints still seed air with no teller. An actual air pickup
  now preserves the chain through raiser to player; testing only `mint_claim` and
  the speaker's stored row would miss the broken initial-air link.
- **Repeated player arrivals reach the receipt writer.** M3 skipped every stage
  arrival whose hop count could not improve the listener's holding. The player
  now reaches `learn` once per `(fact, mouth, game stir)`, including unchanged and
  first-hand-refused merges. NPC stage propagation retains the old skip. The
  player's memo clears on a new stir and has a strict insertion-time bound of
  `FACTS_MAX_LIVE * STAGE_HOP_MAX_PAIRS`, derived from existing limits. When full it
  accepts no further receipts until the next stir; it never evicts and replays a
  pair in the same stir. The roll, pair cap and cadence are unchanged.
- **Witnessing a coded mint is a receipt too.** Ordinary `mint` already gives an
  in-earshot player seeded first-hand knowledge. It now also calls `learn` at
  hops zero, whose immutable-witness merge refuses replacement while the one
  receipt writer records what was seen. This adds no percept or inbox write. The
  self-subject exclusion still prevents a receipt about the player's own arrest;
  M5 owns its deliberate stranger exception.
- **The receipt records the hearing place.** A6's sample copies canonical
  `fact.place`, which would reveal the hidden true event location alongside a
  garbled heard sentence. The stored place is instead the player's area when
  hearing the telling. A regression separates canonical event area, garbled area
  and hearing area. The merged closest telling supplies the retained word and
  provenance; repeats count without replacing them. Even a fresh arrival that
  immediately evicts itself keeps its heard sentence in the receipt.
- **Standing reheat calls `stir_up` only when heat can rise.** M2's generic
  `stir_up` intentionally advances an existing row's stir even when already warm.
  M4's standing path first checks for absent or colder air. A new row starts on
  the current game stir, as D28 requires; a cold existing row advances once;
  repeated speaking at the reheat floor advances nothing. M2's generic method,
  amplification behavior and cadence tests are unchanged.
- **The sheet cap is applied where the keys are recorded.** `relevance_seated`
  retains M1's full deterministic relevance result. `note_seated` takes its first
  `KNOWN_SHEET_MAX` keys, exactly the relevance bullets the sheet can show. This
  preserves M1's API and its existing multiple-relevant-fact regression.
- **Disabled holding readers follow A4 and D49.** M1's relevance test also
  asserted that `holds_key` could read a fact with knowledge disabled. A4's new
  body explicitly returns `None`; D49 gates systemic readers. That assertion now
  checks `None` and separately confirms the raw fact remains stored. A4's prose
  prediction that no M1 assertion would move overlooked its added ablation
  guard. The library relevance test and integration T28 both had this expectation;
  both now check disabled reads and retained data, including restored holdings
  after re-enabling. Their preceding relevance and hidden-block assertions passed.
- **There is one live stake slot.** `standing_lines` selects the newest live
  qualifying claim by sequence, then counts that word's mouths once. Multiple
  claims cannot emit many multi-line blocks into the HUD. The returned vector
  leaves the planned future clock slot available. The live word names the wards
  and the action the player can take there.
- **All new retained allocations count toward the store guard.**
  `footprint_bytes` includes occasions, raises, seated keys, player receipts and
  the player repeat memo. The hot `learn` wrapper uses the existing indexed
  `world.is_player(actor)` check rather than scanning for `world.player_id()`.
