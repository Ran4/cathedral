## Defect fixed: the two mouths could be nearly forty metres apart

`hop_on_stage` selected everyone within twenty metres of the player, then allowed
every selected carrier/listener pair. Two mouths on opposite sides of the player
could exchange a direct telling while outside each other's hearing range.

The pair now checks the same inclusive, full-3D twenty-metre boundary as speech.
Positions are resolved once per selected body; the new check adds no city scan.
Both regression tests failed on the original code before the fix:

- `a_stage_hop_respects_the_distance_between_its_mouths`: a listener at a pair
  distance of **20.000002 m** incorrectly received a hops-1 holding. The test also
  covers exactly twenty metres, a vertical offset, and the ±19 m case.
- `a_stage_hop_needs_an_intermediary_across_the_player`: the far mouth incorrectly
  received the word on the first pass. With the fix, the first pass reaches the
  middle mouth, the second reaches the far one at hops 2, and `chain()` returns
  middle → witness.

## Cost claim corrected

M3 step 5's **432 rolls** is a carried-row bound, not an absolute bound.
`holdings_of()` also returns seeded witness rows, which are deliberately exempt
from `HOLDINGS_MAX`. Nine selected bodies × eight listeners × `FACTS_MAX_LIVE`
gives **18,432 candidate rolls** as a conservative absolute upper bound.

The source comment now states both bounds. The eight-telling cap and the
two-real-second engine gate remain unchanged. Introducing another roll cap would
change which facts can propagate; this review does not make that design change.
The candidate bound is independent of the crowd size. A separate player-centered
scan and its nearby-result sorting still run once per due pass, as D24 specifies.

## Handoff and commit descriptions differ from the numbered decisions

Two claims in `a1381b1`'s commit message and the initial CLAUDE handoff do not
describe the implementation or its authoritative plan:

1. **The substituted actor need not belong to the holder's `knows`.** D16 and M3
   T20 explicitly permit an unknown substituted actor to render as a trade/ward
   role. `person_word` emits a real name only for the reader or someone the reader
   knows; the substitution itself draws from the fixed authored ward/trade pool.
   This preserves reconstruction when the player's `knows` changes. The existing
   unknown-subject and unknown-teller tests cover the privacy boundary.
2. **Layer 2 uses its own gated scan.** D24 explicitly rejects mutating
   `attention::on_stage`: night checks call it as a read, and the ordinary All
   scheduler mode does not call it. The new
   `the_engine_throttles_stage_hops_in_both_idle_modes` test introduces a fact
   after the initial poll, verifies no pickup in 199 intervening polls, and
   verifies the due pickup at two seconds, under both All and Stage modes.

These are description corrections, not behavioral departures from D16/D24.
Likewise, `chain()` does not read the current `Drift::via`: the pickup snapshots
that value into `Held::from`, and the walk follows those recorded mouths. The
garble can then be recomputed from each mouth's own stored hop count.
