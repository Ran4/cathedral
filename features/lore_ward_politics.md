# Ward Politics

**Status:** the lore layer is implemented (July 2026); the gameplay layer below
remains open.

## Lore — DONE

Canonized in **`lore/core_lore/ward_politics.md`**: each of the eight tax
wards has a reputation it claims as a virtue and its neighbours repeat as an
insult, one or two current material grievances, and two named benchers for
F.437. The reckonings (quarterly, with the bencher-choosing **head reckoning**
after Gaudry's Audit), the Sitting custom (candidates sit a plain public bench
while householders state complaints to their faces — one *sits* for the
Bench), **bench-fare** (bread, herring, and small ale as the lawful limit of a
candidate's table), support through meals/favors/kin/credit/guild
pressure/visible service rather than speeches or parties, the
bencher-till-the-grave courtesy title, and the interlocking **Drain Question**
of F.437 (three funding proposals, each blocked by the ward it lands on) are
all in that file.

Companion edits: `core_lore/core_lore.md` (Common Bench in the compact canon +
load list), `core_lore/secular_government.md` (cross-ref),
`core_lore/calendar_and_history.md` (head reckonings),
`core_lore/sayings_and_customs.md` (four new sayings),
`second_sun/07_what_everyone_knows.md` (injectable "The wards and the Bench"
paragraph — every citizen knows all eight ward insults), and anchor lines in
the family stubs of the twelve newly named benchers (Hobbe, Pike, Kett, Bram,
Nett, Mere, Toll, Crake, Sedge, Wren, Dask).

The Reed Ward's two benchers were already canonical (`lore/the_dry_boatmen.md`
and their character sheets): **Idonea Tarn** and **Hamel of the Reach**,
platform postern hours + the Serle tun. The twelve benchers named for the
other wards are seeds (a ward, a trade, a position each); character sheets on
the Reed pair's model may follow, respecting the roster's dispersal rules.
Bell-and-Sluice's second seat is held by a deliberately unnamed Marle
(consistent with `lore/families/family_marle.md`); the F.437 First Seat is
**Gude Dask**, who already had a sheet
(`lore/characters/civic_officer/p009p_gude_dask.json`).

## Gameplay — OPEN

The player could carry evidence, expose a concealed interest, repeat or
correct campaign gossip, broker support, or simply discover that helping one
ward disadvantages another (the Drain Question is built for this). Election
results need only change small, legible things — gate hours, stall licences,
watch routes, shoring orders, well repairs, or enforcement of the Cut game
(deliberately undefined; it is NOT the Dry Race — see
`features/design_the_cut_game.md`, and note the lore doc intentionally never
mentions it) — to make civic life feel responsive. Recurring reckonings would
also create public gatherings and evolving disputes that belong to the Common
Bench rather than the Church.
