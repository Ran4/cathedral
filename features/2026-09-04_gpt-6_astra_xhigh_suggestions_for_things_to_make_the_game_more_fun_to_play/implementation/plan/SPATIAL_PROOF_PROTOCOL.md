# What a route experiment can actually establish

The discovery should be physical: a place the player thought was separated turns out to be connected. Its supporting mathematics must survive the controls, geometry, witnesses and running clock that the game actually uses. This contract belongs to M4/M5/M7/M8/M14/M15.

## 1. Keep three claims distinct

1. **An executed journey:** this body, under these conditions, completed these observed acts in this measured interval.
2. **A historical inference:** the experiment is applicable to the relevant past configuration, access and supported sighting interval.
3. **A development fairness certificate:** the authored collision/movement world has no omitted legal public alternative fast enough to defeat the intended inference.

The first does not automatically establish the second. The third is an omniscient developer check and cannot automatically teach an investigator an undiscovered doorway. Every in-world conclusion needs its own supported observations, plans, inspection or testimony. The notebook never receives a developer certificate as free author truth.

## 2. Select the numerical acceptance relation before tuning geometry

Each candidate/report names its start/finish regions, physical act sequence, allowed body/controller profile, access assumptions and timing units. Export a machine-readable record containing:

| Quantity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[Gmin, Gmax]` | Conservative interval between the last supported initial counter sighting and first supported renewed sighting |
| `Uprivate` | Upper end of measurement uncertainty for one valid complete private execution, including all required work inside the interval |
| `Lpublic` | Certified lower bound on every relevant legal public execution under the declared approximation and historical access assumptions |
| `m` | Advance-selected separation margin, initially at least **2 simulation seconds**, enlarged where the uncertainty budget requires it |
| Configuration | Geometry/portal, body/movement, fixture, relevant stock/occluder and observation revisions |
| Coverage | Which witness/result supports each required act; which claims remain conditional or unobserved |

Accept the intended strong relation only when:

```text
Uprivate + m <= Gmin
Lpublic       >= Gmax + m
```

`Uprivate` proves the measured permitted execution fits; it is not an upper bound on every future attempt through arbitrary crowds. Record a deterministic enactment of the actual authored historical conditions separately from today's repeatable demonstration. Use repetitions to find fragility and measurement variation, not to claim a finite maximum proves all future runs.

The old illustration gives `48 / 2.1 + 8 + 3 = 33.86` seconds before newly modeled stair/door/turn costs. It fails the conservative minimum of 32 seconds, and with a two-second margin needs at least 35.86 seconds even before those extra costs. The old 576-metre graph circuit gives 48 seconds at a horizontal sprint ceiling of 12 m/s, but that is not a certificate for the actual collision world. These are design arithmetic, not measurements of Tallage.

Do not round an overlapping interval into success. An uncertain result can remain inconclusive and permit another method. If a plausible district cannot satisfy the intended strong relation, do not manufacture invisible blockers or falsely award public impossibility. Keep M14's strong-claim gate unaccepted, document the concrete candidate failures and assess an explicitly narrower authored inference before M15 relies on it. The separate physical identification chain must remain logically valid either way.

## 3. A bounded conservative public-route method

M4 supplies a development analysis separate from ordinary NPC routefinding. Build a finite approximation over mandatory passage/approach regions such that **every physically usable route maps into the approximation**, with transition costs that **underestimate** actual time. Shortest time in this permissive approximation is then a valid lower bound, even if too weak to prove the desired claim.

For the first implementation, support a small authored set of regions/cut boundaries with conservative horizontal-distance costs and explicitly validated transition coverage. Include outside-district exits/re-entry, roofs, falls, jumps, stairs, doorway approach areas and direction-dependent transitions. It is acceptable to give an uncertain transition zero cost or add a permissive shortcut; it is not acceptable to omit it because the intended NPC graph lacks an edge.

Document the coverage argument and compare it against the real controller's collision dimensions, maximum horizontal speed, jump/fall rules and all usable openings. A 3D path length divided by horizontal run speed is not necessarily a lower bound. A sparse A* path length is normally an available route's cost, not proof that a free-moving player cannot cut a corner.

The certificate is unavailable if the approximation's coverage cannot be justified. Its weakness is useful design information: the site may need a different ordinary architectural arrangement, or the claim may not be supportable. Keep it versioned and invalidate it on changes to relevant geometry, movement rules or access assumptions.

## 4. Measure sightings and the mechanism causally

Author four potentially different events: last counter visibility, hoist stroke start, stroke completion and first renewed counter sighting. Lise has real positions, eye orientation and travel into/out of the yard. The gap includes bounded travel and recognition delay; the stroke duration alone cannot substitute for it.

M7/M8 provide an ordinary controllable mechanism with inspectable stroke endpoints, rate/range, load class and interrupted/running state. Current decorative beam animation is not this authority. A recorded historical account must establish the comparable configuration and absence of a material interruption. Repeated cycles establish only today's behavior unless those conditions are supported.

Treat relevant differences between the historical and present configuration as **irrelevant, conservative, material or unknown**, with reasons. Moving stacked goods may restore a sightline Lise lacked. A new doorway may shorten today's circuit. A newly unlocked passage cannot establish historical access. Preserve the original observations instead of rewriting them to agree with current geometry.

For Warin, author supported cry-alignment uncertainty and continued beam-side observation over the relevant interval. The observer's exact author timestamp is not available as magical human precision. Establish that his possible departure/return windows and the fastest permitted beam-to-encounter journey cannot fit the supported assault/cry relation. Two independently rooted descriptions of the same cry still need compatible location/time coverage; two copies of one description do not supply it.

## 5. Observation has temporal coverage and observable detail

M5 cannot certify continuous sight from two positive endpoint samples. A packet may pass behind a pillar, a door may close/reopen, or an exchange may happen between them. Process motion and portal changes in temporal order, using conservative swept coverage or sufficiently subdivided checks whose unresolved intervals break continuity. Catch-up endpoints cannot retroactively witness intermediate acts.

Observation receipts distinguish detected person, recognised person, seen generic bundle, supported object features and established object identity. A private `ItemId` is a simulation reference, not an identity the observer automatically knows. An observation track can acquire a stronger identity through uninterrupted handling and later examination, preserving the earlier uncertainty.

M5 supplies the minimal authoritative descriptor for existing held props: exposed/carried/concealed state, support anchor and observable shape/features. M7 extends it for placed/contained evidence, object revisions and multi-step transfer phases. Perception and host rendering consume compatible descriptors. Seeing an actor does not mean seeing every item in their inventory. Current hand rendering chooses only selected held/offer props, and its transfer animation lags the ledger; these seams must be reconciled before surveillance can produce evidence.

Define detection versus recognition/detail ranges, vertical gaze and effects of distance, darkness, fog and intervening surfaces. Use the existing weather visibility data consistently. Lamplight can remain atmospheric without granting a perfect object identity through darkness or making the only intended visible handling impossible to read.

The authoritative receipt must not claim the player saw a transfer that rendered wholly offscreen or behind a panel/occluder under the declared observation policy. UI access to an already learned record is separate from new sensory acquisition. M5/M10 must document whether an open readable panel leaves a visible world aperture and which observations that aperture permits.

### Delayed speech recognition

Ordinary microphone capture records an utterance interval and its input purpose under the runtime generation. During capture, accumulate bounded hearing coverage from the speaker's accepted trajectory and event-time listeners/openings. At completion, freeze that receipt rather than retain an unlimited city transform history until the provider responds. The source currently samples speaker position at `RecordingFinished` and chooses listeners at transcript application; a misleading comment describing recording-start position is not evidence of historical audience capture.

Use a declared conservative policy when recognition supplies only a whole transcript: a listener with verified coverage of the complete utterance may receive the complete proposition; partial/uncertain coverage yields only the supported partial content, or a generic heard-speech observation where word timing is unavailable. Do not give the whole confession to someone who arrived for its last syllable. If a later backend supplies aligned words, its validated timing may refine the captured intervals through the same service. It must not manufacture missing acoustic coverage.

Semantic recognition may arrive later than the speech. Preserve observation time separately from the time its usable content is applied; do not retroactively rerun a guard's intervening decisions. Emit each semantic speech/knowledge effect once through the utterance identity. NPCs and the player can respond to already available information while recognition is pending. Current listeners/doors at callback time cannot replace the captured audience.

Bound recording duration, candidate sets, interval segmentation and retained completed/pending records under M0/M5 budgets. On saturation, preserve conservative uncertainty and a legible interrupted/limited-capture result; never claim full coverage or keep arbitrary histories. Explicit unsent dictation has no public utterance receipt. An interrupted uncommitted capture restored from a save becomes available unsent text/status under [CHECKPOINT_PROTOCOL](CHECKPOINT_PROTOCOL.md), without automatic broadcasting or pretending unknown words were recognised. Already committed speech effects remain historical.

Acceptance pairs include entry/exit or door changes during capture, the same changes only during provider latency, moving speakers across rooms, only final-syllable coverage, cancelled dictation, recording limits and capture/recognition boundaries around save/load. Authoritative recipients may differ from the player's known audience; privacy badges and action availability cannot reveal an undetected listener.

## 6. Cover the acts of a demonstration

Bind every claimed intermediate act to direct observation, a subsequently verifiable physical result, or a clearly identified participant account. Mott watching departure and return does not personally observe a hidden desk pause and rack concealment.

A stand-in returning on time after skipping hidden work may establish a narrow return-time claim. It cannot establish the complete itinerary. The activity service's omniscient completed-step log is developer evidence about execution, not automatically a witnessed statement available to the institution.

Use small clear roles: the player may observe a key landing, a second witness may watch the rack, and the examiner can inspect the actual result afterwards. Do not make seven-person escort micromanagement the price of testing one route. Supported recordings/accounts can replace attendance only where the procedure permits them.
