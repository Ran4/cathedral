# M10 — Alternatives and aftermath

From: old M17 alternatives ([archive](archive/M17_alternatives_and_recovery.md)), the rest of M18
([archive](archive/M18_lasting_consequences.md)) and the host line-of-sight ray. R10 in
[PLAN_REVIEW](PLAN_REVIEW.md). Entry: M9.

Every GDD route becomes reachable, in reduced form where the review said so, and the consequences outlast the case.

## Scope

- **Private admission.** A voluntary confirmed admission with an independently supplied detail ("Leave the pledge" /
  "Then neither"), delivered under seal. A detail established as leaked defeats it; the physical chain stays
  available.
- **Hush and restitution** as M7 agreements with distinct parties: hush is Corin → player, restitution is
  Corin → Warin. Sealed delivery to Mott breaks the hush terms.
- **The four GDD side routes (Q12)**, in reduced form:
  - sponsorship as an effect of Warin's release;
  - E13 surveillance, with a host line-of-sight ray for continuous observation (a wall or exchange breaks it);
  - Warin's cart favour as a one-use agreement;
  - the not-supplied admission detail.
- **Reopening.** A closed inquiry reopens on new evidence without reseeding or resetting other outcomes; a corrected
  finding recalls its order (M7).
- **Aftermath.** Relationships and later dialogue follow what each person learned; Lise may grant or restrict passage
  access (M6 grants); the tools return only on genuine redemption or a valid release.

## Not in scope

Disclosure-history diagnostics, relay windows, an adversarial matrix, a universal reputation score.

## Dev fixture (D01) and lockstep (D06)

Agreements, surveillance and reopening first pass on the dev fixtures. Lockstep scenarios: an agreement before its
deadline, surveillance mid-observation, and a reopened inquiry.
