# M9 — Statements, evidence and findings

Provide the reusable reasoning machinery for an investigation. It evaluates what was submitted and supported, not whether the player guessed the author's answer.

## Entry

M8 and the shared knowledge feature are accepted. M5 observation receipts and M7 examination/custody results are available.

## Records and authority

An inquiry binds an incident/reference, participants, submitted bundles, requested findings, authority decisions and amendments. A submission is an immutable receipt of what was actually lodged, when and with whom. Editing a draft later cannot change an earlier hearing's input.

A recorded account contains speaker, proposition, scope, source basis, time of observation where known, time of statement, recipients and confirmation. An account may be false. The record proves that a person made it, not that the underlying event occurred.

Keep original utterance/transcript references where available, but use a typed confirmed proposition for consequential evaluation. An ambiguous confession cannot be expanded into unspoken acts. Preserve earlier inconsistent accounts rather than overwriting them with the latest version.

Pin the proposition actually confirmed and its disclosure receipt while referencing shared fact identities. Later gossip correction cannot rewrite a lodged account. Retention does not enlarge ordinary NPC prompt budgets.

Register confirmed-account/submission adapters with M8's activity/policy service. An informed NPC may choose or be assigned to lodge their own supported account while the player is elsewhere. Validate against that actor's holdings and authorised records; an uninformed actor cannot submit author truth. Ship versioned declarations and loader validation with these services.

## Evidence model

Evidence entries reference shared fact IDs, observation origins, physical objects, examination results and custody chains. Distinguish direct observations, hearsay, authored documents, authenticated copies and player hypotheses. The UI's arrangement is not another authoritative fact store.

Independence uses origin/event/source relationships established in the admissible record. Two known retellings of one witness are one originating source. Two statements from the same person do not become independent because they use different words. Distinct methods can corroborate one another without being treated as two independent witnesses. Keep actual causal provenance for world simulation separate from provenance established to the reviewer; an unseen retelling cannot become an omniscient diagnostic. An apparently independent but secretly correlated source can mislead a finding until the chain is exposed.

Retention preserves referenced facts/receipts across gossip eviction and save/load. Superseding or correcting a statement keeps its history and changes its evidential status; it does not erase a public action that already occurred.

Register inquiry/account roots and required transitive source links with M5's shared knowledge archival API. Preflight protected-history quotas, compact closed-inquiry transition chatter without destroying submitted cores, and preserve identity when cold events are reactivated. Test beyond live-news/working-holding caps, saturation, amendment and reopening. Do not keep evidence alive by expanding every citizen's prompt or filling all standing-fact slots indefinitely.

## Disclosure and private corroboration

Record who received a confirmed proposition, through what channel, at what time and under what access scope. Use these receipts for public/private submissions, confidential agreements and checks that a corroborating detail was independently supplied.

Implement the private-detail verification service here: compare the later confirmed account with an earlier restricted record, verify their sources and established disclosure history, and return only the supported comparison result to an authorised requester. An established leading question or prior public disclosure can invalidate claimed independence. Do not certify omniscient absence of contamination: an unlearned secret leak cannot change official diagnostics. The service cannot read a hidden culprit bit or expose the sealed detail as a missing-clue hint.

Record confidentiality is separate from spoken audibility. Sealed delivery grants selected record access without broadcasting its proposition. Spoken presentation and confirmation follow ordinary hearing and cannot certify that nobody else can hear. A privacy condition change perceived by the player or explicitly reported by a participant may interrupt an as-yet-unemitted controlled presentation or delivery. Preserve the draft and explain the known change. An undetected listener alone cannot alter availability, warnings or interruption results; preserve their actual hearing receipt for subsequent consequences and learned disclosure history.

Ordinary free speech can already have disclosed a proposition before the player reviews a formal action. Cancelling that action never retracts committed speech. Conversely, typing or dictating into an explicitly unsent account field creates no disclosure merely because the draft contains a true claim. Drafts and resolved references remain knowledge-limited under M10.

M8 agreements consume established disclosure events for fulfilment/breach. They do not inspect a player's private intentions or automatically assume everything typed into a draft was spoken.

## Bounded rules

Provide typed, inspectable rules for supported findings. Initial predicates cover:

- An authenticated identified object or document field.
- A verified match with an explicit method and object revision.
- A dated observation/interval and its uncertainty.
- Independent source origins with the required relation to one event.
- A current or historical access fact, as the rule specifically requests.
- A supported route attempt or valid lower-bound certificate.
- A confirmed voluntary admission of named acts plus independent corroboration.
- Contradictory confirmed propositions about the same relevant circumstance.

Rules compose conjunctions and alternatives with bounded depth and reference validation. Bind typed actor, object, incident and interval variables across the conjunction: matching unrelated valid fragments of different disputes cannot satisfy one finding. Copies retain their original source; methods declare the revisions and identity they actually compared. Do not add a general scripting VM or arbitrary private-world query. The evaluator's input view must omit culprit identity and unsubmitted/unlearned world state.

Results name the supported finding and limited deficiencies. They must not reveal an unknown clue or silently assume its existence. Partial findings are valid: recovery, corrected alibi, release, theft support and assault support can diverge.

## Review and amendment

An authorised reviewer receives the bundle, schedules necessary examinations through M8 and records the supported decision. The model can phrase reactions; the decision service owns the actual grounds.

Findings can request further inquiry, a search, a release or referral. M11 maps those into scoped institutional authority and orders. A general trial/sentencing simulation is not required, but a result must state its procedural meaning accurately.

New evidence can amend a finding. Preserve the old decision, new grounds and correction time. Downstream orders receive an explicit change/recall event, not an invisible mutation of their original supporting bundle.

## Development cases

Use two data-authored disputes: one with independent witness clearance and no identified offender, and one with authenticated property plus an alternative admission route. Their evidence structure must differ from the final case, including a correlated-rumour trap and a corrected statement.

## Tests

- Remove each essential support in turn; the corresponding finding downgrades.
- Add hearsay, a guess, duplicate origins or an unsubmitted secret; no unsupported finding appears.
- Mix individually valid support from different incidents, wallets, packets or named holders; shared bindings reject the mismatched bundle.
- Change only unlearned planting, whisper or retelling history; identical admissible input gives identical findings and diagnostics until a new receipt establishes the difference.
- Verify the evaluator can run without access to the full world or hidden culprit record.
- Distinguish present access from historical access, generic resemblance from identity, and recovered property from an identified thief.
- Distinguish uncorroborated continuous presence from demonstrated opportunity and an actually contradicted account; an innocent subject who stayed through a blind interval is not awarded a lie finding.
- An admission of theft alone cannot substantiate assault; involuntary/unconfirmed/vague statements do not satisfy the admission predicate.
- Save/load and case amendment retain original submissions and each decision exactly once.
- A closed inquiry can reopen without reseeding evidence or resetting unrelated outcomes.

## Completion gate

Both independent disputes produce correct partial/full findings through the same rules and services. The player-facing deficiency view respects knowledge boundaries. M11 receives supported decisions and M10 receives readable, provenance-preserving projections.
