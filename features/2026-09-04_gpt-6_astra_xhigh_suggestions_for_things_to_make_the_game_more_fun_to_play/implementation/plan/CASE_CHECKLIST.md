# What the finished quest must let a player do

## The world it starts in

- [ ] The crime already happened (D02): one person took Odo's packet and shoved him in one continuous encounter, the
      day before the world opens. Nothing is spawned when the player first asks.
- [ ] Warin is held at the Tallage station on recorded grounds, with an expiry in game time. A deferred review never
      extends it.
- [ ] Corin's two counter sightings are real; Lise did not watch the counter between them. The unseen interval has
      its own root: a visible hoist cycle the player can time, and the tenders' testimony.
- [ ] Odo's prior account links the packet removal, the wallet catch and the shove to one encounter, without naming
      Corin. Lise's deposition identifies Corin's wallet (intact before, broken after) and his particular desk.
- [ ] The genuine twelve-spark pledge is unpaid; the tools stay in Lise's pledge custody.

## Findings the player can reach, each from its own support

- [ ] **Alibi corrected:** Corin's continuous presence is uncorroborated, not disproved.
- [ ] **Opportunity:** the timed private route plus historical access; today's open passage alone is not enough.
- [ ] **Warin cleared by witnesses:** Averil's and Gile's independent observations at the cry. Two retellings of
      Averil are one source. Stopping here is a legitimate outcome.
- [ ] **Property recovered:** the authenticated packet with its handling; a similar bundle is not enough.
- [ ] **Theft and assault, physically:** Odo's account plus the wallet chain (fragment origin, fracture match,
      before/after dating), all bound to the same incident, wallet and packet.
- [ ] **Theft and assault, by admission:** a voluntary confirmed admission plus an independent detail or material
      corroboration. A vague or leading-question confession is not enough.
- [ ] **False collection authority:** the document comparison; handwriting resemblance alone is not enough.

## Routes that must each play through

- [ ] Early recovery from the loft rack, no confession, no surveillance (core, M9).
- [ ] Warin cleared with no Corin case (core, M9).
- [ ] Late: miss retrieval and review, then lawful wallet comparison, then desk search, without a bargain (core, M9).
- [ ] Private admission under seal with independent corroboration (M10).
- [ ] Watching Corin's Lamplight retrieval; losing sight breaks continuity (M10).
- [ ] Hush (Corin → player) and restitution (Corin → Warin) as separate agreements; sponsorship as a release effect;
      Warin's cart favour as a one-use agreement (M10).
- [ ] Explore first, learn the meaning later, with nothing reseeded (M10).

## Consequences carried out by the city

- [ ] An order against Corin is carried out by guards who learn it, search, find, seize, escort and commit him, or
      who stay visibly blocked (D07).
- [ ] Warin's release actually happens; the tools return only on genuine redemption or a valid release.
- [ ] People's later behaviour follows what each of them learned; Lise's passage grant or restriction is a real
      access state.

## Throughout

- [ ] The city never pauses for reading, typing, talking or menus (D05); a missed review still happens on what was
      lodged.
- [ ] The interface separates intention, request, acceptance, progress and result, and never reveals an unknown clue
      or names the culprit (D04).
- [ ] Save and load at any point duplicates no object, payment, hearing or arrest, and never reseeds the case (D06).
