# A case worth choosing to pursue

The player should leave Tallage with a place understood differently, somebody helped, and an opinion about what they chose to disclose. Reusable systems make those experiences possible; successfully operating them is not the dramatic reward. This companion to [M19 acceptance](M19_acceptance_and_release.md) describes what humans must experience before technical completeness earns release. It follows [DECISIONS](DECISIONS.md) and the corrected [CASE_CONTRACT](CASE_CONTRACT.md); it does not replace their evidence, scheduling or fulfilment rules.

The [original manuscript](../../manuscript.md) offers a useful rhythm: notice, ask, imagine, try, reconsider, disclose. Preserve that rhythm through [M10's interface](M10_conversation_and_interface.md) and [M14's district](M14_tallage_district.md). A player should choose a walk because two places seem unexpectedly connected, or approach a witness because their account could help Warin. Repeatedly visiting a clerk solely to advance an invisible requirement is a reason to revise presentation and ordinary service access.

## Moments that earn the next choice

**Someone is losing a working day.** Near the beam, Averil argues while other business continues. Warin worries about the load he left secured. The player can ask what happened, follow Averil's invitation to see where she stood, or leave. Specific work and affection give a reason to intervene without compulsory acceptance or a quest announcement declaring innocence. If the opening is missed, the current people and accessible record establish what remains at stake; the injury never replays for the player's benefit.

**The counter disappears.** Lise shows the hoist operation from the yard. From her working position the player cannot see the counter they just left. Her two sightings can remain completely honest. The satisfying thought is “She could not have watched him all that time,” followed by a new question about the interval. It is not “Lise lied” or “Corin definitely left.” Keep Lise's reply particular: she can insist on what she saw while conceding what she inferred. Give the player room to defend her accuracy without endorsing the stronger alibi.

**That wall belongs to the shop.** On the public walk, a recognisable shutter, roof seam or hoist sound connects two views. Upstairs, the service passage makes sense as storage access between working properties. The player chooses to inspect because they have noticed a physical possibility. Discovery works equally well before the accusation, and it adds learned geography without granting entry permission. A consenting stand-in can enact the journey while the player watches the critical doorway. The demonstration earns understanding of opportunity; identifying who committed the encounter still requires independent evidence. If repeated traversal becomes a chore, retain valid results and improve the first demonstration instead of requiring another sprint.

**A catch becomes part of an encounter.** On the early physical route, an authorised rack inspection recovers the packet before retrieval. The player can ask Odo to authenticate it and request comparison of the logged fragment with the specifically identified wallet. Make the examination visible and readable: origin, physical match and intact-before/broken-after dating each answer a different uncertainty. Odo's already recorded account connects the wallet struggle to the same packet removal and shove. This is an identification route without confession or surveillance; finding papers in shared storage alone is not. The player's reward is being able to explain how the objects connect to the acts, rather than watching a suspicion bar fill.

**Warin is enough.** A player can instead check Averil's and Gile's independently rooted observations of Warin at the relevant cry and lodge only that case. Once the correction reaches the keeper and release actually occurs, Warin checks his work and Averil can return to hers. Their relief is the payoff. His authorised cargo-journey favour is usable on its stated terms, not a gifted cart. The notebook can say “Warin cleared on the lodged witness accounts,” with the other inquiry separate. Stopping here is legitimate. Continuing should come from curiosity about the upper rooms, concern for Odo, or interest in the disputed papers, never a completion percentage withholding credit.

These are proposed encounters, not mandatory scene order. [M16](M16_investigation_and_public_review.md) owns the supported routes. NPCs can also submit evidence and clear Warin at an unattended review. On discovering this, the player should recognise people acting for themselves and decide whether other questions still matter; absence does not reserve a rescue for later.

## Choices that alter relationships and disclosure

A public presentation lets people inspect supported claims together. Lise may defend her own account, Gile may become quieter about his pupil, and Odo may insist on a precise correction. Those reactions must follow what each learned. The square continues its work around the review. Avoid a universal applause scene, forced attendance by every witness, or an accusation selected merely because a character portrait is available.

A legitimate private submission protects record access while preserving a full supported account to Mott. The eight-spark restitution is **Corin → Warin**, for lost work; it buys neither immunity nor erasure. After the agreement is accepted, a submission preview can read: “Submit the selected accounts and examinations to Mott under seal. Corin owes Warin eight sparks; payment outstanding.” A completed payment needs its own receipt. Where admission is used, confirmation and independent corroboration remain necessary. Private-detail verification uses established disclosure history; the interface cannot expose an unlearned secret leak as a failed-independence warning. “Mott received the confirmed account under seal” records semantic receipt; it does not mean Mott automatically observed the hidden act described, or that others heard it.

The hush offer has a different temptation: **Corin → player, eight sparks**, for withholding a named packet/account bundle until the specified next High Wick deadline. Show the exact known records and date before acceptance, including “Sealed submission of this bundle to Mott also breaks this undertaking.” Show explicitly excluded material, such as Warin's independent cry-witness accounts. This permits separate clearance of Warin while the Corin inquiry may remain unresolved or mistaken. It is a compromised bargain, not a privacy setting. Refusal leaves the physical route available. Acceptance cannot recall already lodged records or silence another witness; breach consequences require established disclosure and ordinary procedure, not a magical deduction from the player's purse.

Document recovery also has a modest, concrete human stake. The genuine **twelve-spark pledge remains unpaid**. Invalidating the forged collector's authority protects the household's redemption right but does not free the plane, brace and chisels. Show, after a supported comparison: “False collection authority rejected. Tools remain pledged to Lise; twelve sparks unpaid.” A later actual redemption or valid release can authorise handover. The player can value preventing wrongful collection without an invented doorstep delivery or treating Warin's separate eight sparks as the pledge payment.

Before private delivery, make its meaning concrete. If the player has freely spoken a sensitive account and then chooses “lodge under seal,” the preview can say: “Only authorised readers can access this lodged copy. People who heard you speak may already know its contents.” Use wording grounded in actual known speech, without listing unknown eavesdroppers. The player can correct the spoken claim and restrict the new record, but neither action erases what was already heard. An explicit unsent draft stays unsent until the player commits its selected channel.

## When the city interrupts

[M17 recovery](M17_alternatives_and_recovery.md) must preserve useful choices when the intended sequence breaks. Reading, typing, evidence selection and menus never pause the city. Retain the page and draft, keep important local changes perceptible, and avoid filling the screen with ordinary operation updates. A known appointment can show its day, office and location. After its time passes without news, show “Scheduled time passed; outcome unconfirmed,” not a guessed verdict.

**Late, or refused.** The player returns after Lamplight and the original review, or Lise declines further conversation and the player refuses Corin's bargain. Consulting the authorised docket recovers Lise's existing deposition, not a newly invented witness. Known inventory, preserved encounter material and the identified wallet provide lawful grounds in sequence for the later desk route. An absent keeper calls for a reachable ordinary access arrangement. A blocked request should offer a specific next action grounded in known material, such as “Wallet comparison requested; examiner unavailable. Arrange attendance.” It must not name a hidden desk packet as a helpful hint. The player learns the actual review outcome; formal clearance, provisional release and detention expiry remain distinct. Deferring review cannot extend the original hold.

**The watcher loses sight.** At Lamplight, a wall or intervening exchange breaks continuous observation of the packet. Show only what was seen: “Corin passed behind the wall; the bundle's identity through that interval is unverified.” Keep useful observations, but do not award uninterrupted tracking from hidden history. The player may seek examination, later testimony or the complete physical chain. Even verified later handling does not alone prove the original theft or disprove yesterday's two sightings. Tension comes from choosing a vantage and recovering after uncertainty, not a concealed surveillance progress meter.

**The order is still pending.** After a supported finding, the player hears Mott issue an arrest order while Corin is elsewhere. The known status can be “Order issued; no arrest reported.” Assignment or search updates require an acquired report. Watching informed guards depart is a consequence; finding Corin already jailed without any physical encounter is not. Let the player return to other business, offer an observed lead, or later consult the record. Do not require shadowing every officer to make enforcement proceed. [M18](M18_lasting_consequences.md) owns the continuing actions and differing personal aftermath.

## Formative sessions and revisions

Plan **five to eight formative sessions**, seeking different familiarity and input preferences, including deliberate slow reading, keyboard controls, typing and voice where available. This is a small design sample. No tester session has occurred. The following criteria are proposed iteration triggers, not measured results, statistical claims or promises about completion time. Use [M19](M19_acceptance_and_release.md) and [VERIFICATION](VERIFICATION.md) for protocol and technical evidence.

Give an unguided opening before any branch-specific prompt. Record the player's chosen goal, observable action, hesitation, help requested and their own explanation before revealing the author solution. Across sessions cover early physical identification, independent Warin-only clearance, private restitution and hush distinctions, late/refusal recovery, interrupted surveillance and pending arrest. Use explicitly labelled production checkpoints for uncovered situations after free play; separate assisted coverage from spontaneous discovery. Nobody must complete every branch.

Use this limited revision checklist:

| Proposed criterion | Evidence to collect | Revision when it fails |
|---|---|---|
| The district supplies a discovery | At least two independent sessions produce an unprompted spatial observation or useful question grounded in the seen yard/passage | Rework sightlines, ordinary visual landmarks or the demonstration. Do not add an unknown-clue marker. |
| The player owns the inference | After relevant encounters, ask what the sightings, route and physical chain establish separately | Any “blind interval proves a lie” or “shortcut proves assault” explanation triggers review of the specific wording/feedback; retest after revision. |
| Helping Warin feels complete | Ask the Warin-only participant what changed, whether stopping feels legitimate and why they would continue | Remove misleading completion framing and strengthen actual release/work reactions. Do not gate the reward behind Corin. |
| Alternatives have understood costs | Before committing, ask the participant to paraphrase recipients, money direction, withheld material, deadline and the tools' unpaid pledge | Correct the preview wherever restitution, hush, privacy or redemption are conflated; do not accept an accidental bargain as agency. |
| Interruptions leave an intelligible next choice | After a missed meeting, refusal or broken observation, ask what is known and what they would try | Improve the grounded receipt, accessible record or recovery service. If no complete continuation exists, return to M17. |
| Consequences remain readable and worth seeing | Ask whether the known arrest status is issue or custody, then revisit one ordinary affected person/place | Correct status wording and learned updates; replace generic ending praise with a reaction or fulfilled action appropriate to that person's knowledge. |

If two independent sessions describe the same stretch as unexplained errands, review its travel, access and conversation together. Combine redundant acknowledgements, preserve accepted work, and move a useful demonstration into the place being discussed. Keep necessary scope and disclosure choices explicit. Record the revision and test that stretch again within the remaining sessions; if coverage or comprehension is still missing, report the human gate as pending. Passing deterministic checks cannot stand in for a player wanting to make the next choice.
