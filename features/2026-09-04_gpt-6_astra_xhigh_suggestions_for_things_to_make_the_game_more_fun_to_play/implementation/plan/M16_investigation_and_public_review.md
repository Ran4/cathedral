# M16 — The complete core investigation

Deliver the first full playable investigation through ordinary city systems. The player can clear Warin independently and can assemble a complete physical case without a confession or surveillance.

## Entry

M15 is accepted. The corrected recipes in [CASE_CONTRACT.md](CASE_CONTRACT.md) supersede the GDD model's incomplete theft path.

## Playable flow

1. **Encounter the accusation.** Warin's situation, Odo's injury and the announced review give the player concrete stakes and ways to act. Establish the hold/review timing without turning it into a quest-mode countdown.
2. **Inspect the room.** Read the account, learn the missing packet's description, understand the desk/upper entrance and examine the logged fragment through actual permission and custody.
3. **Hear the alibi precisely.** Lise's two sightings and Corin's stronger continuous-presence statement remain separate. Let the player inspect where she stood and where her view failed.
4. **Check Warin independently.** Averil and Gile relate the relevant cry to his work position. The player can lodge that support and stop after a legitimate clearance.
5. **Walk and discover.** The street journey establishes the apparent separation; the upper connection changes the possible route. The discovery can happen before or after the witness conversations.
6. **Demonstrate a limited claim.** Use the hoist/view position and a player or stand-in route attempt. Observers acquire the actual result. A result establishes feasibility/observation limits, not guilt.
7. **Connect the physical act.** Authenticate the packet and complete the fragment origin, wallet match and intact/broken dating chain. Odo's independent removal account connects the identified wallet to both acts.
8. **Request a supported finding.** Lodge a selected bundle, choose disclosure scope and present the relevant physical demonstration/account. Mott records what is supported and what remains unresolved.
9. **See the city respond.** A release/order/property instruction enters M11/M12. The player sees actual fulfilment when present, or learns it later through a real record/report.

## Public review

Build the meeting through M8 invitations, travel and attendance. Only necessary participants are required for a requested finding. The player selects the claims and evidence; the scene does not require reciting a password or dragging all seven people across the map.

Use real demonstrations: Lise's blind position, a route attempt, the catch comparison or document authentication. Keep the world moving around them. A missing operator, blocked door or urgent institutional duty can interrupt a demonstration and yield a specific continuation.

The original scheduled review can occur without the player. It uses actually lodged evidence, including valid NPC submissions. A player who takes longer requests a later/limited finding or reopens the record; the review does not silently wait until their walkthrough reaches its final page.

## Player comprehension

Make partial success visible. “Warin is cleared on these witness accounts” can be a complete achievement even with an unsolved theft. “The route fits” is useful without “Corin did it.” “An arrest order has been issued” is distinct from “Corin has been taken into custody.”

The interface shows accepted submissions, scheduled/pending actions and actual results. It does not reveal a hidden clue to explain a deficient bundle. Character reactions communicate loyalty, embarrassment, fear and relief without replacing the underlying evidence requirements.

## Acceptance routes

- Two-witness clearance with no Corin evidence and no forced continuation.
- Early packet recovery followed by complete physical theft and assault findings, with no confession/surveillance.
- Passage discovered first, then interpreted through accounts.
- Corrected alibi and opportunity only, with a properly limited finding.
- Missing one origin/match/time/removal link: the affected allegation remains unsupported.
- Public authentication that protects unrelated private pledge information while preserving the necessary proof.
- Player absent at review, then reading the actual record and continuing.

## Tests and play evidence

Run these paths under fake cognition and through the normal UI. Then test live conversations for relevant answers, refusal to invent, distinct voice and reaction to the player's actual disclosures. Provider wording failures cannot mutate the authoritative result.

Save/load before and after every essential examination, submission, finding, release order and arrest-order issue. Verify that M12, not a quest callback, carries out enforcement.

Use formative human play to ask for the player's own reconstruction. The three intended discoveries are the unseen interval, the architectural connection and the difference between opportunity and identification. If the notebook does the reasoning for them, revise the presentation before proceeding.

## Completion gate

A player can complete the core case through at least the two required independent routes, with real spatial actions and institutional consequences. This is the early full-playability milestone, not completion of the overall requested scope. M17 adds the remaining approaches and recovery paths.
