# M9 — The core case (D01 review point)

From: old M15 incident and cast ([archive](archive/M15_incident_and_cast.md)), M16 core investigation
([archive](archive/M16_investigation_and_public_review.md)) and the core of M18
([archive](archive/M18_lasting_consequences.md)). R9 in [PLAN_REVIEW](PLAN_REVIEW.md). Entry: M8.

The quest becomes playable from a fresh world to Warin's release and Corin's guard-executed arrest. This is the D01
review point: the developer plays it before alternatives are built. [CASE_CONTRACT](CASE_CONTRACT.md) is the content
contract; [CASE_CHECKLIST](CASE_CHECKLIST.md) is the outcome list.

## Scope

- **One established history (D02).** A case pack installs the cast roles, the crime's fixed history, the documents
  (pledge, false release, counterfoil), Odo's E01 account and Lise's deposition when the world is created, with the
  incident's calendar anchor resolved and stored then. Restore never seeds (D02); a late developer start advances from
  the same anchor rather than creating a new one.
- **Cast.** Corin, Lise, Odo, Warin, Averil, Gile and Mott, with Mott promoted to a major. Ordinary work continues
  around the incident.
- **Warin's hold.** Warin is held at the Tallage station on recorded grounds with a game-time expiry (M7), not through
  the Stone House "prisoner" circumstance. Deferring the review cannot extend it.
- **The packet.** In the loft rack at opening; Corin's Lamplight retrieval is an M6 dated errand that a learned
  warning can change.
- **Core routes** ([CASE_CONTRACT](CASE_CONTRACT.md) playthroughs 1, 2 and 5): early physical recovery with the
  wallet chain; Warin cleared by the two independent cry witnesses with no Corin case; and the late wallet-then-desk
  route after missing retrieval and review.
- **Core consequences.** Warin's actual release, the order against Corin executed by guards (M7), and the packet's
  custody and return.
- Content lives in data. The no-case-IDs grep stays clean.

## Not in scope

Private admission, hush, restitution, sponsorship, E13 surveillance, the favour and reopening (all M10).

## Dev fixture (D01) and lockstep (D06)

The M4–M7 dev fixtures keep passing with the case pack loaded and with it absent. Lockstep scenarios at the decisive
boundaries: before retrieval, between lodging and review, and with Corin's arrest in progress.

## Acceptance (player-visible)

1. From a fresh world, the main path plays to Warin's release and Corin's arrest by a guard who actually reached him.
2. Clearing Warin with the two cry witnesses alone is a complete, acknowledged outcome.
3. A player who misses retrieval and the review still reaches a lawful desk search and the packet.
4. A review the player misses decides on what was lodged, including what NPCs lodged themselves.
5. Saving and loading at each decisive boundary duplicates no object, payment, hearing or arrest.
6. The developer plays it and their notes drive revisions before M10.
