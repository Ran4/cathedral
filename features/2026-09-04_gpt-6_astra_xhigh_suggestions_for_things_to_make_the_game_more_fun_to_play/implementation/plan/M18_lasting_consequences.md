# M18 — Life after the finding

Make the outcome visible in subsequent ordinary gameplay. A lasting consequence is a record and an action carried out by people, not only an ending paragraph.

## Entry

M17 is accepted. Reuse M11/M12 for law, M8 for undertakings, M7 for property and the shared knowledge system for what spreads.

## Separate outcome records

Track Warin's detention/release/formal correction, packet custody, theft support, assault support, document/collection authority, disclosure scope, restitution, hush obligations and rewards independently. Multiple combinations are legitimate and can be amended.

An order may remain issued while Corin is unfound. Corin may be physically held while a later review is pending. Warin may be provisionally free but not formally cleared. The UI and dialogue must say which state actually applies.

## Ordinary fulfilment

- **Release/correction:** an authorised instruction reaches Warin's keeper; the relevant detention ends; restraint is removed and actual exit assistance permits his ordinary schedule to resume. Other valid legal grounds and a physically obstructed exit are handled explicitly.
- **Arrest:** informed guards execute the order through actual search and movement. The player can encounter the process or learn its completed result through a source.
- **Property:** the packet goes to named custody with independent ownership/return claims retained after prisoner release. The tools remain under the genuine unpaid pledge until actual redemption or another valid release permits a real handover/delivery. Rejecting the forged collection authority does not cancel the debt.
- **Money:** accepted restitution/hush obligations pay once from actual funds or remain outstanding with a clear cause.
- **Favour:** Warin's thanks becomes a defined authorised cargo-delivery undertaking, with availability, destination and one redemption. It does not gift a cart he does not own.
- **Access/work:** custody, keeper absence or a damaged door affects ordinary work/access; use existing recovery/cover policies rather than a quest-only substitute actor.
- **Earned passage:** Lise may grant supervised/daylight future access or restrict her discretionary permission according to established cooperation/disclosure. Apply actual M6 grants/revocation; learned geography remains and independent lawful scope still works.

## Relationships and news

Author bounded personal consequences for the seven cast members: gratitude for clearing an accusation, resentment of needless public exposure, Lise's loyalty under corrected facts, Gile's response to his pupil, and Corin's response to disclosure/breach. They depend on what that person learned and how, not universal access to the player's final choice.

Public notices and rumours can spread incomplete or corrected accounts through the shared system. The original accusation and its amendment remain distinct. A correction need not instantly erase every distorted retelling, but durable institutional authority must reflect the current disposition.

Night reflection may alter tone and future personal aims. Skipping a provider night cannot reverse custody, delete evidence, repay an obligation or rewrite a finding.

## Return visits

On returning after an office/day or after loading, show the player's last established result and known pending work. Let them consult the docket, ask the relevant people or inspect changed places. Do not provide omniscient live tracking of Corin or the packet.

Reopening adds evidence and amendments to the existing history. It does not rerun the opening injury, reset the packet, restock reward money or summon every actor back to a scene marker.

## Tests and longitudinal evidence

- Follow every representative outcome for several offices/days with the player elsewhere.
- An outstanding arrest with stale leads remains pending honestly; later real sighting can resume enforcement.
- Recall/release prevents rearrest under the same executed/discharged order.
- Packet/tool/money/favour fulfilment conserves objects and funds and emits one receipt per completed act.
- An unpaid valid pledge remains after E16 rejects the client's collection authority; no unsupported free tool-return order is issued.
- Different cast knowledge produces different aftermath responses without leaking secrets.
- Ordinary trade/needs/housing still work when Corin or a keeper is unavailable.
- Save/load before and after fulfilment, night reflection and case amendment preserves the same continuing city.
- NPC initiative may resolve some pending business without the player; records explain what happened.
- Return after a day/load with Lise's conditional grant or restriction: actual passage access, supervision/expiry and retained geographical knowledge agree with the learned outcome.

## Completion gate

All outcomes have executable fulfilment and return-state behaviour. The quest can become history without freezing its actors in an ending scene. The full case is now functionally delivered, subject to M19's acceptance evidence.
