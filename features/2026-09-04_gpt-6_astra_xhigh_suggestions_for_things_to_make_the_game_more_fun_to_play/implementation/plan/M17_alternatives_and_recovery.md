# M17 — Alternatives and moving evidence

Complete the GDD's alternate approaches on the same systems. The investigation must survive changed order, slow reading, missed events, unwilling witnesses and a suspect acting on what they learned.

## Entry

## Private account and restitution

Offer a private submission through the same evidence/authority process as a public one. Corin may voluntarily confirm specified acts. Verify the private detail against its independently recorded source and disclosure history, or use the dated physical chain. A bare admission does not substitute for corroboration.

Privacy controls actual recipients and access to records. It does not erase an assault or protect the suspect from already submitted evidence. Keep unrelated household details and Lise's private circumstances out of public disclosure unless the player intentionally makes them relevant.

Restitution is a real agreement from Corin to Warin for eight sparks of lost work, alongside a full supported sealed submission. Reserve spendable funds and transfer once. A shortfall remains explicit; dialogue cannot conjure eight sparks or buy erased assault grounds.

## Hush bargain

Author a separate explicit hush offer with its own payment and promised disclosure behaviour. It is a compromised player choice, not a button named “private resolution.” Acceptance does not erase records already lodged or stop another witness acting independently.

Use the finite terms in the case contract: Corin pays the player to withhold a named bundle until a named High Wick deadline, including withholding it from sealed institutional submission. Distinguish material excluded from those terms, such as Warin's independent cry-witness accounts. The player can still clear him through that separate route while the Corin inquiry lacks withheld support. A repayment demand after breach is not an automatically enforceable court order; ordinary procedure judges its grounds.

Track fulfilment, breach and what each participant learns. The player can accept and later disclose, or refuse and continue. Consequences arise from observable conduct/records, not an omniscient morality meter. A repeated conversation/review cannot pay again.

## The packet retrieval

Extend M15's already-installed real rack-to-desk movement at the announced Lamplight. Add the case contract's bounded warning/delay/alternative-entry behaviour with actual M14 routes, M6 permissions and M8's learned-trigger strategy service.

If early recovery, custody, a blocked route or another holder prevents pickup, record the failure and leave the packet where it actually is. If interrupted mid-transfer, preserve the last completed location. Do not run an invisible later move to force the planned ending.

The player can plan observation through real positions and learned timing. Continuous object identity and sight are required for E13. Losing sight produces an incomplete observation, even if the private simulation knows the bundle's identity.

Retrieval after a denial can support a contradiction/knowing handling. Original theft still requires its own act-link, admission or the complete physical route. Observing a player-initiated handover to Corin is not proof of an earlier theft.

## Late and official routes

Author passage, rack, wallet and desk requests separately. The late physical route uses the authenticated missing-object inventory and supported relevant grounds; it cannot require possession of the missing packet to authorise looking for it.

Mott/keeper absence uses ordinary institutional access and meeting policies. A lawful alternative may take time, but cannot be an unimplemented promise. Refusing a bargain must leave at least one complete physical investigation path.

Missing the original review yields its actual record. A supported deferral moves the meeting, not Warin's hold expiry. The player can seek provisional release/sponsorship separately from formal exoneration and return for a later finding.

## Adversarial and recovery behaviour

Handle moving/planting objects, repeating claims, presenting only selected facts, swallowing a small fragment, returning the packet, alerting Corin early, blocking a door, abandoning a demonstration and returning several days later.

Evidence remains physical and knowledge remains perspectival. A hidden planting cannot be rejected because the evaluator secretly read the full item history; only submitted/established defects can support that rejection. Conversely, the engine's object identity prevents a generic substitute from passing an actual unique comparison.

## Tests

- Private corroboration succeeds on established support; an established leading disclosure defeats independence, while an unlearned secret leak cannot become an omniscient diagnostic.
- Public/private/hush choices have distinct recipients, promises and payments.
- Early recovery prevents scheduled pickup; delayed/abandoned attempts conserve current location.
- Walls and exchanges break surveillance continuity, with a complete alternative still available.
- Late player, no confession, no surveillance: lawful desk search completes the physical case.
- Menus/reading/typing span retrieval and review while unrelated city activity continues.
- NPC autonomous submissions can change an unattended review; player absence is not a hardcoded outcome.
- Repeated review/reopening/load does not duplicate payments, objects, statements or orders.
- Unique evidence survives permitted bodily/inventory handling or carries explicit transformation lineage.

## Completion gate
