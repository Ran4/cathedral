## Current source findings

- The four cohort slots in checkpoint/budget.rs are exclusive and Reservation
  is linear. Independent budgets are not complete-save admission.
- The legacy aggregate meter charges every lexical key/container. Its historical
  backbone-plus-Round expanded estimate exceeds 128 MiB; those recorded component
  bounds remain unchanged. A new closed typed-layout proof is being designed.
- World::export_backbone_checkpoint rejects nonempty World.events before export;
  its diagnostic cost method does not. Engine::poll's final flush drains via
  mem::take and then invokes state-mutating handlers. Complete capture needs the
  real ordinary-boundary invariant, never an extra drain or poll.
- EngineClimateDtoV1 already binds both processed clock/weather cursors exactly
  to the saved clock position. WorldClockDtoV1 nevertheless permits scale up to
  1,000,000 and seconds_per_day as low as one. A 100 ms accepted frame can span
  100,000 days. offices_crossed_days builds its entire Vec before the Engine
  trims bell catch-up; forced_lightning_crossed also builds all crossed strikes
  before reducing the output. Finite endpoint checks alone do not establish
  next-frame allocation/CPU safety. Ordinary scheduled lightning already bounds
  its search to three days and must not be described as the same unbounded path.
- Round also retains last_game_days, production_last_game_days and optional
  last_office_days. Its existing component validation bounds each independently.
  production_overlap_minutes loops across every crossed day, while
  tick_food_economy collects every office since its own cursor. A bounded current
  clock rate alone cannot make an arbitrarily old saved cursor safe. Complete
  validation needs bounded next-consumer spans that still admit legitimate
  cadence delays and continuous scale changes. Weather semantic_revision_at
  separately has a fixed 32-day search guard.
- Ledger counter exhaustion is intentionally valid when the ordinary allocator
  safely refuses. New full validation must preserve that distinction, rather
  than reject all MAX counters or normalize valid Never/history values.
- Backbone currently allows world_revision/event_sequence at i64::MAX - 1;
  World::touch_public_state and World::emit increment without overflow handling.
  A complete ordinary poll can increment these repeatedly. Their next-pass
  headroom needs an explicit complete contract; one remaining increment is not
  a bound for the combined consumers.
- PlaceRegistry's checkpoint loader rebuilds four HashMaps and clones their
  keys. An accounting wrapper that sees only serde sequences/maps cannot assume
  it has charged those custom allocations automatically.
- unique_set consumes SeqAccess while constructing BTreeSet, and KnowledgeV1
  converts wire Vecs into tree maps/sets and Arc stores. The same sequence token
  shape therefore does not establish a Vec-only allocation bound. Derived stores
  coexist with unconsumed wire buffers during conversion.
- The inspected flatten attributes on Engine count records are serialization
  diagnostics, not checkpoint decoding paths. GeneratedRoutineV1 and host
  RecordV1 have tagged buffering paths that require their own accounting audit.

## Public seam and allocation proof gates

Before independent tests, agree a supported full capture/decode/candidate API.
Tests will use exact canonical raw equality, incompatible component mixtures,
mandatory schema/manifest roles, actual loaded-definition identity and shared
budget lifetime/cancellation. No trusted test constructor should bypass the
public malformed-input path that production exposes.

The proposed private metered decoder must prove collection size_hint behavior,
first Vec allocation and growth, BTree root/split/alignment costs, string/escape
scratch and all custom/newtype/record decoder/index paths before allocation.
Inline struct/tuple storage differs from dynamic containers. Cumulative complete
expansion cannot decrease when a stage drops; live retained and working charges
may decrease only after their actual owners are disposed.

Source maps, final checks, library/layout evidence, real complete authored and
+2,000 workloads, release tails and acceptance are pending. No Cargo command or
production edit was performed by the coordinator during this initial review.

The initial complete manifest implementation uses fixed digest/scalar fields and
only BoundedText<4096>, BoundedText<256> and BoundedText<128> for owned strings.
Its outer ordinary serde parse can therefore use the precharged fixed allowance
plus raw escape scratch; this conclusion does not authorize arbitrary future
dynamic manifest collections. The source review identified navigation.bin and
lore/places/ombreval_buildings.json as production embedded inputs missing from
the first build-script selection. Their source path strings alone do not bind
the compiled input bytes.

The Running allowance is a trusted caller admission contract, not heap
introspection. A named 512 MiB minimum does not prove all arbitrary Engines or

authored/+2,000 authority scope, including retained capacities and explicit

must jointly apply the documented interruption policy without rejecting valid
saved authority merely because these independently meaningful states differ.
