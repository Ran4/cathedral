Orphan household/place anchors are source-reachable history. The public snapshot hides unresolved sites immediately; only the next eligible ordinary sweep removes their authority. A catalog swap also need not remove earlier kinds/anchor pairs. Decode therefore does not require live actor/subject/author existence, current anchor resolution, current catalog acceptance, about-equals-anchor, or uniqueness of `(kind, anchor)` after low-level insertion. The exact current ordered registry/home and shelter definitions are still bound and validated against World or an unadopted BackboneCandidate.

Disabled marks retain their authority and continue weathering. The ward's once-per-day beat can remain spent while every mark has been scrubbed: that empty store must not be replaced by a default. No separate cleaner worker exists in Marks; ordinary scrub plus spent-day re-chalk suppression is the actual covered cleaning contract. The private Round continuation test exercises zero-to-one and saturated twelve-to-twelve notching and source-choice penalties through the existing private writer/reader.

`decay_scale` and its initial Engine config copy serialize as IEEE u64 bits. Config/CLI accepts NaN and infinities; ordinary sweep already interprets all nonfinite/nonpositive scales as 1.0 without updating their stored value. This v1 representation preserves payload NaNs, infinities, negative values and signed zero exactly. Other stored mark floats use supported finite domains; catalog nonfinite numbers are refused before fingerprinting.

`WorldMarksDtoV1` and `EngineMarksDtoV1` provide admitted export/decode/encode and opaque candidate validation with read-only accessors. There is no public partial install API. Private component-only installs scramble all covered authority, use independently driven control/restored World and Engine prefixes, assert immediate canonical bytes, then compare ordinary decay, cleanup, refresh, scrub, daily redraw, allocation, publication, source selection and revision behavior. New fixed fixtures cover populated sparse/history/disabled World state and an Engine with spent beat, consumed ID, an empty clean wall and a real publication cache.
