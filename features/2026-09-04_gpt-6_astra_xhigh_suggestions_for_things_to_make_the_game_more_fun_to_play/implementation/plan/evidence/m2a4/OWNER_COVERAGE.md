# M2a4 owner coverage — 2026-09-08

Status: Implemented component cut; final focused/public/workspace checks pass. Coordinator release review and commit pending; complete M2 remains in progress. Base M2a3: `31ec2a36506c09bfd538708eb28f4ee6c7bab575`.

This cut adds three explicit components: private WeatherTimeline, sampled World climate/context, and Engine clock/weather authority. Candidates are opaque and have no production World/Engine replacement method. M2a1–M2a3 retain their accepted coverage. There is no complete save envelope, capture command or adoption path.

## Supported numeric policy and unresolved temporal work

All weather anchors use finite calendar values within the existing ±1,000,000-day format range; the accepted logical range remains 0..1,000,000,000 seconds. The M2a1 clock rate limits are numeric format policy, not demonstrated CPU-safe effective rates.

WeatherClimate's public `with_climate` assigns arbitrary values, while this v1 format deliberately supports finite nonnegative scalars at most 1,000,000 and minimum duration no greater than maximum. This is a narrower supported-format policy, not a claim that every finite custom runtime climate is accepted. Ordinary sampling still applies its actual 0.25..8-hour wet-duration clamp; decode preserves the supplied supported knobs. Initial WeatherConfig frequency may be finite in ±1,000,000, with resolved timeline frequency nonnegative. Sample unit/wind/visibility bounds match the production sanitized output.

Two existing catch-up paths remain explicit full-envelope CPU gates: `ring_offices` materializes all `offices_crossed_days` before retaining two crossings, and forced `lightning_crossed` enumerates a long span before retaining the newest useful strike. This cut does not change either ordinary path or certify the other day/office consumers. Bell next-pass counter headroom is not a complete clock/accounting lifetime proof. These constraints must be resolved before M2b adoption.

## Mandatory composition still pending

`Engine.movement_now`, `next_round_tick_at`, accepted host debt/residuals and the complete capture/dispatch boundary still need their combined owner contract. Round's accepted private component does not own these Engine cadence fields. All other Engine temporal state remains saved/pending with its actual owner: `last_player_sound_at`; conversation/NPC exchange/novelty anchors; scheduler, floor, speech and Night obligations; `next_stage_hop_at` and `next_player_pollen_game_days`; door/journal timers; retained diagnostics/transcript and publication revisions. None is reset or silently omitted from an apparently complete Engine envelope.

World knowledge/facts/salience/ward moods, law/custody/notices/marks, dogs, and remaining switches/catalogs remain pending. General geography validation still must bind place/home/patrol/worksite/generated identities to a complete World manifest, reconstruct `area_adjacency` under its knowledge contract, and validate downstream catalog/resolver references. The area/sound fingerprints here do not satisfy those separate contracts. M2a1 behavior/generator/hash/build compatibility must compose with all these owners before adoption.

The next proposed coherent cut is knowledge/facts/pollen authority with its saved geography/area-adjacency contract, followed by conversation/attention and law owners before scheduling/speech composition. No M2a5 begins before this cut is accepted and committed.
