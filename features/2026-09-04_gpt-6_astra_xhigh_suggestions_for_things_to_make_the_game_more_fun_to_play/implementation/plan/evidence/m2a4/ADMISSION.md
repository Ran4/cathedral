## Weather validation scratch and CPU bounds

Recomputing a scheduled sample has working memory beyond a tiny DTO's lexical charge. `semantic_revision_at` collects 129 quarter-day knots and up to 35 days of two six-boundary wet episodes plus two fog edges: `129 + 35*(2*6+2) = 619` doubles. Its initial 512-slot Vec grows to at most 1,024. A conservative full second 1,024-double allocation covers stable-sort scratch. Per-episode semantic boundaries are a six-double stack array.

The water integration materializes at most 16 episodes, at most 17 showers including residue (charge capacity 32), and at most 17 banked pairs (charge 32). On this target Episode is 64 and Shower 24 bytes. `2*1024*8 + 16*64 + 32*24 + 32*16 + 4096 = 22,784` bytes covers these buffers and extra validation/error scratch together, below 65,536. Scheduled searches have a fixed 32-day semantic guard and six-day wetness window. Forced drying stops at its fixed exhausted exponent. No validation path enumerates crossed office/lightning events.

Installed context hashing streams borrowed definitions into SHA-256 with a 4 MiB encoded limit per context family. It never builds owned arrays or reparses trusted definition files. Limits bound traversal: 4,096 areas, 1,024 boxes per area, and 25,000 sound/ambient rows each. Nonfinite public area/sound values are rejected before JSON could collapse them to null. Shelter storage is constructor-validated and immutable through its read-only API; navigation uses its saved original-input fingerprint. Existing external geometry/catalog storage belongs to its supplied Running/candidate context and is not claimed inside this standalone payload budget. No separate trusted-definition parser scratch is needed.

The 65,536 bell-deadline count is a supported component cap, not a proven maximum under every possible legacy clock/poll cadence. Maximum count and equal ordered deadlines are exercised directly with attached decode/candidate admission. Whole-envelope effective-rate and accounting horizons remain pending; bounded validation does not certify ordinary catch-up work.
