# Speech input admission

These are conservative requested-allocation/lease bounds, not an allocator-fragmentation or process-RSS measurement. Global caps remain unchanged: encoded J <=128 MiB, lexical expanded E <=128 MiB, depth <=64, shared Running/SavePayload/LoadCandidate/RetiringGeneration <=1 GiB. Each speech lease reserves `4096 + 4*E + 3*J + 4194304` bytes. The meter allocates no input-derived strings/maps/rows and scans full raw input before typed decoding. Canonical encode/candidate conversion never shrink original raw padding/escape charges. Admitted owns projection before lease, so allocation drops first. Candidate conversion moves the same data without cloning.

E charges 512 per object/array, 64 per scalar/string/key and twice every original encoded string byte. Four E cover typed/projection copying and container growth; three J cover original bytes and serde_json escaped-string geometric scratch. Input-derived unknown-field/error strings are charged by E/J, not a claim that arbitrary errors fit fixed framing. Private wire/remote records perform duplicate/missing/unknown/type/variant rejection; required nullable adapters prevent absent Option fields silently becoming null. Public DTO/row records have Serialize only.

The additional 4 MiB covers saved ledger validation at SpeechCheckpointContext::validate: no candidate ledger or World is constructed. Prior ledger limits are 256 roots and 4,352 receipt IDs/ordinals. A deliberately loose installed BTree node bound assumes one full node per entry: `16+12*8+11*(key_size+value_size)` bytes. OperationId 16 B, CommandId 24 B, ordinal 8 B yield `256*288 + 4352*(376+200) = 2580480` bytes. Adding 65,536 B of fixed recursion/serializer/error framing stays below 4 MiB. Actual nonroot nodes contain at least five entries. Ledger sets are dropped when validate returns, before all subsequent projection scans. Each owner/receipt/key scan then borrows at most eight accepted/capture/stream rows; pairwise duplicate/root counts allocate nothing. Saved receipt lookup scans borrowed ledger entries without copying and exact receipt comparison borrows all text.

Preflight/export serialize StateView directly from source. No temporary Vec or JSON Value is built. Transient stream_jobs/timings/tts_backends are neither scanned nor copied; arbitrary old capacity does not enter the captured projection. After admission and source validation, source strings are cloned at populated length. Captures and streams collect exact-size iterators; the accepted batch+parked chain has at most 8 rows and its minimum/growth capacity is covered by row object charges. A receipt copy is part of that charged row, including at most 2 affected strings. Export validates projected receipt fields after the copy as well, without a second projection or ledger.

Standalone supported counts are independently 8 captures, 8 streams and 8 combined accepted rows. Empty vectors allocate nothing. A singleton capture/stream four-slot minimum fits its 512 B of E in the outer array; a singleton accepted row needs 992 B for four 248 B slots, covered by the outer array plus that row's mandatory object/key/scalar charges. Nonempty geometric capacity is at most twice populated rows after the singleton minimum. Capture String 24 B doubled =48 B per scalar, below 64 B of E even empty. Stream and AcceptedRecording concrete layouts are checked against 64/512 B respectively: a stream row contributes an object+two keys+text; an accepted row contributes its object, eight required keys, nested position/semantic/receipt/deadline records and string payloads. Even doubled accepted capacity fits comfortably below its lexical charge. AffectedRef 48 B doubled =96 B versus object plus key/text charges. Empty vectors with historically huge source capacity produce only populated rows. Stdlib/source/layout evidence is retained separately.

Basenames bind existing check_basename/is_valid_id/MAX_ID_CHARS (128 Unicode scalars, at most 512 UTF-8 bytes), bare path and case-insensitive .wav suffix. Captures/streams are individually unique; accepted basename/request strings may repeat and sibling command steps may share one operation. Requests admit 65,536 UTF-8 bytes including empty/control/Unicode. Available unsent text admits 400,000 UTF-8 bytes, including text exceeding ordinary PLAYER_SPEECH_MAX_CHARS (500) or containing forbidden speech characters. These are supported-save bounds, not new ordinary behavior limits; no normalization/truncation is performed. Count maxima plus maximum text independently remain subject to aggregate caps.

Task positions use the prior supported city point domain: finite and max absolute component<=1000000. Grace/deadline provenance preserves every IEEE bit using required `{bits:u64}`, including NaN payloads, infinities and signed zero; no consumer treats them as loaded timers. Receipt time is finite logical time<=boundary, bitwise exact including -0; receipt text/count/reference domain matches the prior ledger owner. Semantic Some iff exact receipt Some; IDs require issued protected roots. Live speech_actions equality and saved reconstructed IDs are validated without a heap set. Full owner/category/payload digest agreement remains the envelope's responsibility because task lacks original spatial_seq and pre-acceptance pose.

Malformed overcounts and overlong strings can be typed only after the full raw E/J lease fits. Their dynamic allocations follow the same row/container charge; validation refuses supported-shape violations before candidate construction. Raw depth/padding exhaustion refuses before allocations, and any failure releases its lease. Private maximum/sparse and malformed tests are separate from ordinary populated Engine timings.

This local proof does not solve full capture/hydration coexistence: the previous naive backbone+Round Save+Load alone is 1,256,093,444 B, above 1 GiB before Running. Full phase/lifetime admission, host scheduling, all-consumer horizons, callback generation fencing and initial publication remain pending. No cap increases or synchronous-host acceptance are claimed.
