### Extension contract, not a claim that an archive shipped

M5 of **this investigation plan** extends the same `knowledge` owner with an immutable archived identity tier and retained observation roots. M9 adds inquiry/account/submission roots; M2 supplies private DTO/validation support and later owners extend it. No other module owns a second canonical proposition graph.

Freeze these admission limits for implementation: 256 hot facts and existing prompt/carrier limits remain; up to 16,384 archived identities, 65,536 provenance links and 4,096 registered protected roots within a 32 MiB archive budget per world. Identity payloads are bounded to 1 KiB and links to 128 encoded bytes; count limits never waive the byte ceiling. Reserve essential installed-pack roots/identities/links before world creation. Shared transitive roots count once; graph validation rejects cycles/dangling dependencies. Optional news may fail admission without rolling back a completed physical act. An inquiry unable to reserve its essential history defers before accepting it. Neither saturation nor a closed case deletes a retained submitted core. Releasing the last root permits compaction; reopening uses retained stable IDs, not a reminted lookalike. Corrections append versioned support rather than changing an old witness's observed past.

These are finite design/admission budgets, **not measured DTO sizes or implemented APIs**. M5 must measure populated archive overhead against the 32 MiB ceiling and exercise >256 mints, >6 holdings, shared/released roots, source corrections and saturation. M9 must test closed/reopened accounts; both extend M2/M3's fresh-process continuation cases. Any incompatible content-pack addition/removal goes through the checkpoint manifest, not `quest_phase` guessing.

## Exact clock, input and migration handoff to M1

`Engine::poll(now, commands)` receives a generic elapsed value; no accepted-time coordinator exists. `tick_movement` can spend at most eight 0.05-second slices and discards larger backlog. Bevy virtual time caps each delta at 100 ms. Moving Engine alone to wall time would let domain/calendar time outrun physical movement. M0's example supplies nominal 0.05-second increments and changes neither policy.

M1 must introduce the shared accepted physical/domain stream with bounded catch-up and retained ordinary debt, preserving fixed residual separately from engine movement residual. Logical time 10 s, 400 ms ordinary debt and 15 ms fixed residual must restore as three distinct values. No closed-app or load-preparation time becomes debt. Controller and sim share the same ordinary boundary; saving does not cause another poll. FIFO action position samples precede a final authoritative physical position sample/watermark. `LocalEngine` currently drains unbounded command/completion cohorts and pumps before the later input collection; capture of an interpolated render transform is insufficient.

Engine's office crossing occurs before command processing and handles its own scale change. **Round and Night Office still evaluate `last_office_now` using the current clock slope** (`round.rs` office tick; `night.rs::poll`). M1 migrates both to calendar cursors, including a rate change during the round's skipped cadence and across midnight. Keep this as an explicit V05 continuation regression, not a rename.

M1b's concrete transactional regression: `actions::take_into_charge` calls `world.custody.seize` before its fallible route-budget computation. Construct a no-route failure after eligible seizure inputs and assert no custody/notice/inventory/event mutation. The whole-runtime generation fence includes typed input, cognition/Night completion, microphone onset/status, STT, TTS/PCM, capability status and presentation acknowledgements; utterance sequence is not a replacement for runtime generation.
