# M19 — Full acceptance and release

Prove the complete delivery across systems, interfaces, long-running play and human understanding. This is the final gate; tests and persistence have already accompanied every milestone.

## Entry

M0–M18 are accepted with their own evidence. Resolve open risks and intentional scope limitations before treating a successful demo as release readiness.

## Deterministic acceptance

Run the full simulation/backend/host suites appropriate to the changed modules. Add an end-to-end scenario matrix covering the core and alternate paths in [CASE_CONTRACT.md](CASE_CONTRACT.md), including insufficient evidence, correlated hearsay, planted objects, refusal, absence, interruption, missed events and dynamic orders.

For each decisive transition, save just before and just after it, restore into fresh services/process state, and continue. Compare committed effects with an uninterrupted control. Include search, escort, hearing, private disclosure, swallowed unique objects, payment and property return.

Verify the evaluator has no hidden-culprit input. Verify unseen actor/object motion does not update player knowledge or guard leads. Verify essential duties continue without stage proximity or cognition completions.

## Spatial and running-game acceptance

Use only `CATHEDRAL_HEADLESS=1` for agent-driven Bevy checks. Follow `.claude/rules/CATHEDRAL_DRIVE.md`; do not use synthetic desktop input or allow a test window to take focus.

Capture the actual public/private routes, stairs, access operations, sightlines, cry witnesses, examinations, review and guard escort. Test both player and NPC movement, different door states and legal alternative routes. A visual capture supplements collision/navigation tests; it does not establish an exhaustive lower bound by itself.

## Never-pause and accessibility acceptance

Keep each panel open long enough to cross meaningful city events. Read, type, use voice, wait for slow STT/TTS/cognition, browse saves and change settings. Clock, schedules, needs, operations and guards continue in all cases.

Test readable text scaling, keyboard-only access, captions, action-picker parity, ambiguous names, focus recovery, interrupted drafts, last-known-location wording and layered reminders. Missing a window cannot silently award its evidence or eliminate every complete route.

## Live-provider acceptance

Measure holders/non-holders answering questions, formal account confirmation, evasive strategies, admission of specific acts, response to disclosed evidence and unsupported accusations. Log prompts/results through the existing private session archive.

A model must not invent a physical result, grant an illegal action, expose sealed history to an uninformed actor or change what a confirmed statement means. If prose reliability is insufficient, tighten the deterministic core clause/receipt boundary while keeping natural conversation around it.

## Performance and durability

Compare default cast and 2,000-extra-citizen play with M0. Use 20,000 as a stress case with its existing cost acknowledged. Measure sim pump, spatial queries, active activities/orders, knowledge retention, snapshot/UI traffic and save capture/encoding/write separately.

Use the frozen workload/limits in [RUNTIME_BUDGETS](RUNTIME_BUDGETS.md), including coordinator staging/retirement/COW, callback queues, prompt/publication bytes and knowledge archive saturation. Do not pick new thresholds after observing the result. Verify production focus scheduling as well as headless operation, and sustained accepted-time debt under the ordinary workloads.

Run multiple game days and repeated save/load/reopening cycles. Check bounds on facts/receipts, inactive inquiries, command dedup state, legal orders, operation logs and provider queues. Bounded retention cannot discard referenced evidence or revive completed work.

Test corrupt/incompatible saves and interrupted writes. Previous valid slots and the running world must survive the documented failure paths.

## Human acceptance

Recruit five to eight formative testers across different input preferences and familiarity levels. Follow [PLAYER_EXPERIENCE](PLAYER_EXPERIENCE.md) for the scene coverage, proposed revision triggers and distinction between unguided discovery and assisted fixture coverage. This is a design feedback sample, not a statistical proof. Ask each to explain their reconstruction before presenting the author solution.

Look for the intended discoveries: two sightings are not continuous observation; the upper connection changes the journey; opportunity does not identify an assailant. Test whether players who clear only Warin recognise a legitimate achievement, and whether players who continue want to do so.

Ask what they believe is happening when a request is accepted, a guard is assigned, an arrest has not yet occurred, a meeting is missed or a search is blocked. If they cannot tell, improve receipts and presentation rather than adding explanatory implementation jargon to the game.

If players merely collect highlighted clues until the notebook supplies Corin's name, the investigation has failed. If the detour feels artificial, revise the district. If the case feels like administrative chores, improve spatial demonstrations and character reactions before adding more clues.

## Release record

Record implemented milestone status, actual commands/test results, source/content versions, captures, human findings and remaining material limitations. Reconcile the GDD/model/walkthrough with the shipped case and the authoritative decisions.

Update feature status and backlog bookkeeping only when the whole feature is implemented under `features/AGENTS.md`. Preserve historical implemented records. Ship regeneration/documentation instructions and a content-author example so a future quest can start from the delivered systems.

The final claim is the full playable quest plus its reusable foundations, whole-world persistence and ordinary dynamic consequences. Do not mark it complete with a core-only slice, a fake-only demo, a paused quest mode or a teleporting arrest.
