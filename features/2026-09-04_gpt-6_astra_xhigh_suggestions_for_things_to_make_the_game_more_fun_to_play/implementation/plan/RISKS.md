## Order of risk retirement

First close the knowledge prerequisite and time/whole-world continuation risks. Next prove real geometry, perception, access and object identity in compact independent places. Then validate evidence, ordinary institutions and interaction together before M13. Only after that do the final site and case consume those capabilities.

The most expensive uncertainty is not the number of authored clue paragraphs. It is whether a live, mutable city can preserve coherent knowledge, physical state and legal work across interruptions and loads. Cutting those contracts to reach the first dialogue sooner would not meet the user's systems-first or full-persistence decisions.

## When a gate fails

Record the concrete failing fixture and narrowest affected claim. Return the defect to its shared owner; retain any separately accepted work. Do not work around it with a case-specific teleport, clue grant, pause or private-world query. For geometry/experience failures, produce a reviewable revised site or interaction and rerun its affected proof and play evidence. For performance, change the workload only through a dated explicit budget revision, not by quietly reducing test population.

A temporarily unavailable renderer or human tester leaves that evidence pending. It does not invalidate useful source/deterministic work, and it does not authorize a fabricated pass.
