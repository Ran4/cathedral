- The Chapter substituted common mourning tolls.

- Their objection is: “One bell is not a name.”
- Families still seek and pay for belated name-knells.

- Markets remain active, but the city is visibly built for three times its current population.
- Property disputes involve dead households, absent heirs, shared walls, salvage, and abandoned upper floors.
- Cloth trade and river work continue at reduced or redistributed scales.

I avoided making every Second Sun document or theory about the Hammering.
