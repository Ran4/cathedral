# Bells as the city's data bus

The two civic bells are the one channel that bypasses the attention gate —
every NPC anywhere hears them. These ideas treat the bells as a writable,
readable, forgeable broadcast medium instead of ambiance.

### False Peals

Let the player climb a tower and pull the rope — and the whole cast believes
the bell. A false curfew empties the squares, a false summons drags a ward
somewhere, and seventeen counted knell strokes make everyone wonder who died
at seventeen. It is one physical lever that perturbs every system at once, the
gravest crime short of violence, and the manhunt afterward runs entirely on
law machinery that already exists. One rope, dozens of heist routes.
