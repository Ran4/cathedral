I / THE CANON CONTRACT

What can be known?

The world stays coherent by distinguishing physical facts from durable interpretations and living rumours. Certainty is local. Ultimate cause remains contested.

◆

Stone

Stable observable fact. The city may deny it; the world does not casually contradict it.

  • The disc requires original rose glass.
  • Its light has measurable consequence.
  • Long exposure can produce counter-memory.
◈

Glass

A strong interpretation, supported by evidence, always capable of fracture.

  • Reserved creation.
  • A world walled from its second sky.
  • An optical instrument or buried civic memory.
✦

Flame

Rumour, doctrine, private grief, useful lie, and story under social pressure.

  • Another cathedral looks back.
  • The canal sees what heaven hides.
  • At conjunction, every oath becomes true.

V / THE PEOPLE IN THE LIGHT

No one is only a belief.

Search by name, role, or allegiance. Every character has an ordinary livelihood, a cross-faction debt, a dangerous uncertainty, and something more urgent than explaining the setting.

9 shown

Sabine Vale

Lucent Warden · Custody

Turns sensation into careful questions, then divides dangerous answers across seven files.

Private fracture: She fears every institution competing to possess the light—including her own.

Tomas Rusk

Master glazier · Saint Lume

Believes a window lives through repairs and that a perfect repair is a lie.

Private fracture: His illicit replacement transmits more wrong light than the original he broke.

Mara of the Lath

Plasterer · Route-keeper

Knows every wall was somebody's paid decision and every opening needs a resident's consent.

Private fracture: Her newest aperture is a chain of hospitality, not a place.

Avel Noll

Flour porter · Absent resident

Remembers a bakery that was no paradise: the rye rent was high and its roof leaked.

Private fracture: She remembers evidence of a murder behind a present family's cellar wall.

Nera Quill

Canal polewoman · Route ally

Measures distance in pole strokes and never says “sun” beneath an arch.

Private fracture: She returned a dry, future-dated viewing token to the black water.

Edrin Pell

Physician · Witness advocate

Does not need to settle metaphysics before treating unusual memory humanely.

Private fracture: Edrin remembers patients who were never born in this city.

Iven Roofless

Roofer · Open Sky radical

Calls roofs buried skies and secrecy obedience delayed; plans to remove the rose's lead.

Private fracture: He has never seen the Second Sun at all.

Hessa Dault

Soap boiler · One Shadow

Names the victims whenever scholars turn harm into an interesting theory.

Private fracture: She sees the pale disc whenever her eyes are closed.
