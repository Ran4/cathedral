This folder develops The Second Sun, suggestion 1 in features/50_cool_suggestions.md, into a setting pillar that can support exploration, conversation, ritual, conspiracy, and long-term social play.

The core image remains simple: through the cathedral's western rose, a second, wrong-coloured sun is visible in a sky that does not exist outdoors. Its light enters the nave at an impossible angle and makes shadows disagree. The city's institutions have spent generations learning how to live beside that fact without ever agreeing what it means.

Reading paths

Start here

  1. Canon and mysteries establishes the stable facts, disputed interpretations, and truths deliberately left open.
  2. The long history of the occluded sun gives the city a shared past, dates, disasters, reforms, and recurring anniversaries.
  3. Faiths, heresies, and institutions turns the mystery into people with motives rather than a single lore answer.

To write or prompt characters

  • Citizen knowledge and NPC prompt bible separates common knowledge, professional knowledge, secrets, and suggested conversational behavior.
  • People of the divided light contains reusable actors, relationships, secrets, wants, and scene hooks.
  • Rumours, sayings, and oral culture supplies truth-tagged gossip, prayers, insults, children's lore, and passphrases.
  • Lexicon and style guide keeps generated text tonally and terminologically coherent.
  • Prompt-ready context packs provides small, composable district, occupation, faction, evidence, and scene blocks.

To place content in the city

  • Sites, phenomena, and relics anchors the Second Sun to architecture, objects, recurring sensory details, and the five squares.
  • Documents of the hidden daylight provides sermons, edicts, letters, trial records, marginalia, inventories, and graffiti ready to appear in-world.

To design play

  • Game design: seeing the unsayable defines principles, verbs, systemic loops, social consequences, accessibility, failure, and implementation stages without touching current game code.
  • Quest and encounter anthology develops authored and systemic situations with multiple approaches and outcomes.
  • Interactive showcase is a visual, filterable atlas of this whole package with direct links to each source file.

Canon contract

This collection uses three confidence levels:

  • Stone — stable observable fact. NPCs may deny it, but the world should not casually contradict it.
  • Glass — a durable interpretation supported by evidence, yet breakable.
  • Flame — rumour, testimony, doctrine, dream, or deliberate lie.

The mystery works only if the game preserves uncertainty about ultimate cause. The player should be able to prove that something is happening, discover who benefits from each explanation, and change the city's relationship to the phenomenon. They should not receive a final codex entry saying which faction's cosmology is objectively correct.

File ownership

Everything in this directory was created as a self-contained design package. It proposes no changes to the Bevy project, Python sidecar, configuration, or other lore directories.
