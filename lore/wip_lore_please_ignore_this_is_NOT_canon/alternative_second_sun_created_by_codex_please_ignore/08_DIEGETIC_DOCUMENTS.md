# Documents of the hidden daylight

This is an anthology of texts that exist inside the city. Spelling is normalized
for readability; presentation can later add damaged edges, rubrication, seals,
handwriting, or missing portions. Bracketed notes are editorial and not part of
the original object.

## I. The public notice at the west doors

**By authority of the Cathedral Chapter and the Office of Lucent Custody**

All persons seeking admission to the western viewing cord shall observe:

1. No glass, lens, polished plate, mirror, powder, oil, or image may be held above
   the shoulder unless stamped for use.
2. No place within the cord purchases an appearance. The token purchases only a
   place.
3. Witnesses shall not seize, turn, strike, shade, accuse, embrace, or measure
   another person on account of that person's shadow.
4. Those experiencing warmth of the left hand, metallic taste, lost interval,
   recollection of an absent street, or grief without present object may speak to
   the grey-gloved lector beside the north pillar.
5. No child shall be questioned as witness without a guardian of their choosing.
6. The words **reserved appearance** are sufficient for public description. No
   worship is owed to light.
7. Touching the glass, lead, scaffold, hanging cord, or western masonry shall be
   prosecuted as injury to the cathedral fabric.

By entering, you affirm only that you entered.

*[Someone has written below: “By leaving, you affirm only that you left.”]*

## II. Canon Orra Mel's thirteenth table, fragment

**Day 201 after the autumn equinox; western cloud; fourteen present by chance.**

At the ninth bell the familiar light stood six degrees above the neighbouring
roof. A colour without place entered the Lamb and the three lower blues. The
second disc was reported by nine, denied by two, not asked of three.

Shadow pegs: ordinary fall east-by-south. Lesser fall north, inclining two marks
while the source appeared stationary.

Warmth: black cloth +2; white cloth +1; bare stone none; Sister Arde's hand +3 by
her report, +1 by instrument.

Sound: west lead sounded E-flat when touched. It ought not to have been touched.

Unexpected: a boy entering after the appearance ended described its lower edge
accurately. He had waited outside and claimed no prior sight. His mother struck
him for lying. I record this not as proof of vision but proof that our method
causes injury when it makes a mother choose between instrument and child.

Conclusion: the light resists becoming an appointment, and perhaps a witness
resists becoming a measure. Tomorrow admit cleaners only. Do not tell them why.

## III. Sermon for Sufficiency Sunday

*Attributed to Canon Leth Arquen; copied by a listener.*

You have heard it said that one light is true and one deceives. You have heard it
said that one is common and one holy. You have heard learned mouths arrange the
heavens as two coins upon a table.

This morning I set one loaf upon the altar.

If the west burns violet and we do not divide this loaf, the light has taught us
nothing. If the west remains dark and we divide it, darkness has deprived us of
nothing. A sign may enlarge our obligation; it cannot replace the person to whom
we are obliged.

Do not mistake me. I have seen stone warmed where no open sky reached it. I do
not ask you to blind yourselves. I ask the harder thing: look, and still remember
the ordinary face beside you. Wonder is a poor neighbour if it consumes every
chair.

The day we have is sufficient for the good we know to do. If another day is
reserved, let it find us doing that good—not queuing to be told we were right.

## IV. The Book of Openings, walking chapter called “Lath and Water”

*This chapter is transmitted as directions. Reading it without walking is
considered incomplete.*

Begin where the saint has one stone eye and rain has the other.

Do not take the lane named on the wall. Take the name beneath it, which the lime
shows after wet weather. Walk until two houses shake hands above you. Thank the
beam for shelter. Name the tax that made the hands meet.

At the cooper's stair ask no question. The person mending the second tread will
tell you a lie. Accept it without agreement. A route is not made only of truth.

Turn toward the smell of blue wool. Pass through the market door only when it is
held for someone carrying more than you. Hold it in turn.

Below the fourth roof that is also a road, look up. The line without a source is
the margin of this chapter.

Question: Where did evening lodge?

Answer: Under the fourth bridge, where no west remains.

If the line is absent, the chapter is old. Do not pretend memory is a door.

## V. Deposition of Enna Voss, cathedral sweeper

**Lector:** State where you stood.

**Enna:** Under the north row, between the pillar with the chipped vine and the
one the pilgrims kiss though they are told not to.

**Lector:** State what you perceived without explanation.

**Enna:** Dust.

**Lector:** Unusual dust?

**Enna:** All dust is unusual if you are paid to move it. This lot went round in
a ring though the door stood shut.

**Lector:** Was there a light?

**Enna:** There are forty-seven lamps, two aisle windows, the rose, and whatever
you call what you have brought me here to say.

**Lector:** Did you see a second disc?

**Enna:** No.

**Lector:** Your first statement says, “It shone behind the blue beast.”

**Enna:** A shine is not a disc.

**Lector:** Your second shadow was observed.

**Enna:** Then ask my shadow. You have more witnesses for it than for me.

*[The deposition ends. A later hand adds: “Do not summon this witness again
without payment for the half-day lost.”]*

## VI. Letter from a mother during the Pale Hands plague

My dear Sella,

I went to the ward against what I promised you. I did not ask the light to cure
me. You must believe that. I lay where they put me because there was a clean
sheet and no room at home to keep the little ones from my breath.

Near evening my hand made two marks on the wall. One was mine. In the other I
remembered you coming through the door in your red coat, though you had no red
coat and were already three years in the ground.

I remember the weight of rain on your shoulder. I remember being angry you had
forgotten onions. I remember forgiving you before you asked. These are not pale
dreams, Sella. The onions were soft at one end. You shook the rain over the floor
and I scolded you.

I am well now. That is the cruelty of it. I am well here and you are alive there,
and no mother was made with room enough for both.

They say running water takes the second mark. Tomorrow I will wash the wall. I do
not know which daughter I betray by doing it.

Your mother, in the city that remains,

Mera

## IX. Broadside sold in Glass Market

### SEE WITH HUMILITY — AND AT THE FAVOURABLE ANGLE

Odo Vey & Company offers lawful aids for contemplation of the western appearance:

- blue-smoked viewing lens in horn frame;
- measuring cord to preserve neighbourly distance;
- paired woad cards for comparison of shadow;
- illustrated guide to the twelve face-shielding figures;
- weatherproof token purse with anti-theft knot;
- family package including one child-height vantage block.

No appearance guaranteed. No doctrine implied. No refund for cloud, silence,
spiritual insufficiency, crowd misconduct, Custody closure, ordinary sunlight,
or disappointment arising from prior exaggeration.

Ask about our canal reflection excursion. The word “sun” is not included in the
price and must not be spoken beneath Saint Orra's Bridge.

## X. Concord of One Shadow handbill

### YOU MAY REFUSE TO BE EVIDENCE

You owe no scholar the contents of your grief.

You owe no priest a useful interpretation.

You owe no merchant a vivid account.

You owe no heretic the wound that proves their courage.

Close the lens. Leave the cord. Wash the mark. Come to the black-lined door in
Soapmakers' Row for food, witness advocacy, burial dues, and a room without
coloured glass.

One shadow is enough because one life must be lived.

## XI. Extract from the trial of the western extraction

**Judge:** Five persons entered the gallery. Each of you names a sixth. Describe
this person.

**First accused:** Tall, though stooped beneath the beam. Hair cut to the jaw.

**Second accused:** A pale burn around the left thumb.

**Third accused:** Spoke as if answering a question we had not yet asked.

**Fourth accused:** Wore the mason's apron of the old pattern, pocket on the
wrong side.

**Fifth accused:** Called me by my mother's private name.

**Judge:** Who admitted this person?

**First accused:** I believed the canon had.

**Canon:** I believed the apprentice had.

**Apprentice:** I believed he belonged because all the others behaved as if he
did.

**Judge:** Did this sixth person touch the glass?

**All accused:** No.

**Judge:** What, then, did he do?

**First accused:** Counted us.

*[The page containing sentence and disposition has been removed.]*

## XII. A physician's instructions for counter-memory

To family and neighbours:

Do not say, “There is no such street,” when the sufferer has already acknowledged
its absence. You add no information and take away trust.

Ask what the place was used for, who lived there, what changed with the seasons,
what they miss and what they do not. Ordinary detail does not prove the memory,
but it reveals distress and need.

Do not visit an alleged absent property without permission of its present
occupants. Knowledge of a concealed room is evidence for a magistrate, not
license for a crowd.

Allow sleep away from coloured light. Provide familiar food. Record new memories
before discussing them with other affected persons, lest agreement be mistaken
for independence or independence be destroyed by agreement.

Never promise return to the absent life.

Never require its renunciation as the price of care.

## XV. Letter never sent by Lucent Warden Sabine Vale

Tomas,

You say I cut the image because I feared what it showed. I cut it because one
whole image can be owned. Seven contradictions require seven thieves to agree.
It was the most faithful thing I have ever done and the ugliest.

The east rose in the parchment is not ours reversed. Count the chapels. There is
one for every chapel we built and one for the chapel Odren left unmeasured. Its
west end opens onto the altar as if the building expects light from inside its
own body.

I have looked at my strip for twenty-six years. The ink changes only when I lie
about having destroyed it.

Tell Mara I know she has the sixth strip. Do not ask how. Tell her I will not take
it. If conjunction comes, bring yours to the chamber of weights and let the
pieces remain one hand apart.

I do not know whether joining them opens a view or closes a wound.

That is the last honest sentence my office permits me.

S.

## XVI. The missing cadence of Sister Belis

*Words written beneath musical notation.*

First voice: Day has one door.

Second voice: Yet evening entered twice.

First: Stone bears the window.

Second: Yet light bears the stone.

First: We are the witnesses.

Second: We are what is witnessed.

Margin: The final note is not sung by a throat. It is the interval between the

## XVII. Property petition concerning an absent door

To Provost Salvi,

We, Tern and Alys Cobb, presently and lawfully resident at No. 8 Dye Lane, ask
relief from persons assembling in our cellar to inspect a door they say belongs
to No. 3 Bellfounders' Steps, which is not a chartered address.

We do not deny:

- the outline in the east wall resembles a filled doorway;
- the claimant Avel Noll knew where the old hinge pin lay beneath plaster;
- knocking produces a hollow return;
- our youngest called her “Aunt Avel” before any introduction.

We deny that these facts make our house a public road.

If the city recognizes absent easement, let it first answer who repairs an absent
roof, who pays an absent tax, and whether present children must yield beds to
people remembered elsewhere.

We request a guard, a mason, and a physician, in that order.

## XVIII. The Open Sky proclamation known as “Against the Hinge”

A door is praised because it opens. Look again: it opens only where a wall has
given permission.

A window is praised because it admits light. Look again: it cuts light to the
shape authority chose.

The chapter says its rose reveals a mystery. Look again: lead crosses the hidden
day like prison bars, and guards sell the right to stand beneath it.

We do not seek another doctrine behind the first. We seek the end of ownership
over sky, sight, passage, memory, and name.

At conjunction, when the gaoler's sun and stolen sun touch, remove one piece of
lead. Not glass. Not stone. Remove the line that says colours must not meet.

Every locked door adds darkness to the Occluded. Every opened roof is one honest
star.

*[Mara-of-the-Lath's response appears below in charcoal: “A roof you remove from
someone else's bed is only another authority choosing weather.”]*

## XX. A double obituary

**In the official register:**

Marten Pell, cooper, died aged forty-one after fever. Survived by husband Joric
and daughter Nen. Buried east ground.

**Pinned beside it in the Keepers' room:**

Marten Pell, gardener of the western tower, remains aged sixty-three in the
memory of Lio Fen, who apprenticed under him in the city of Bellfounders' Steps.
Survived there by no husband and two sons. Known for pears, bad singing, and a
left knee injured falling from the absent bridge.

We record neither life to cancel the other.

## Use in play

Documents should rarely exist only to be read once. They can be:

- carried to an actor who notices wording the player missed;
- read aloud, causing nearby people to hear and react;
- compared with a testimony or physical site;
- forged, censored, damaged, sold, returned, or publicly posted;
- withheld to protect someone;
- prompt context that lets an actor quote or misremember a line;
- evidence of relationships through handwriting, ink, or who possessed the copy.

The most valuable document creates a choice about circulation. Truth in this city
is not only discovered; it is given an audience.
