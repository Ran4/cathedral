# The Wicks

> *A nondescript house that shares the ward's name.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** thin-spread — no ward more than one or two
- **Trades:** domestic service, cobbling, fine metal, general labour
- **Anchors:** none of note

The Wicks share their name with a whole ward and the chandlers' trade, which
makes them the easiest name in the city to overlook — a scatter of servants,
labourers, a cobbler and a fine-metal hand, with no ward to call a heartland and
nothing in the record. A useful *blank* family: a name that could belong anywhere
and draws no attention, for a bit-player who should read as pure background. (Take
care not to confuse a person named Wick with Wick Ward or a candle-wick — the
overlap is exactly why the name hides so well.)
