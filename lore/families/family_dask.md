# The Dasks

> *A plain, hardworking, unremarkable house.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice and Weigh streets
- **Trades:** droving and animal-work, boat-work, cobbling, service, civic office
- **Anchors:** Gude Dask (20, one of Bell-and-Sluice's two benchers and, in
  F.437, First Seat of the Common Bench — chosen precisely because a Dask has
  no enemies worth naming; see `../core_lore/ward_politics.md`) — otherwise
  none of note, and that is the point

The Dasks are the roster's baseline: beast-handlers, a couple of boatmen,
cobblers, servants, scattered through the central and weighing streets, and
*not one* recorded crime, feud or faction among them. A family that simply
works and goes home — which is how, when the Drain Question deadlocked every
bencher of weight, the Bench's chair went to the plainest house in the city.
Cast a Dask when a scene needs a plain, honest, load-bearing citizen with no
angle — the ordinary Ombrevaler against whom the schemers and heretics stand
out.
