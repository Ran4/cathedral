# The Dasks

> *A plain, hardworking, unremarkable house.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice and Weigh streets
- **Trades:** droving and animal-work, boat-work, cobbling, service, civic office
- **Anchors:** none of note — and that is the point

The Dasks are the roster's baseline: beast-handlers, a couple of boatmen,
cobblers, servants, one small civic officer, scattered through the central and
weighing streets, and *not one* recorded crime, feud or faction among them. A
family that simply works and goes home. Cast a Dask when a scene needs a plain,
honest, load-bearing citizen with no angle — the ordinary Ombrevaler against whom
the schemers and heretics stand out.
