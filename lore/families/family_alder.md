# The Alders

<img src="crests/family_alder_crest.png" alt="Arms of The Alders" width="150" align="right">


> *Kept the boat.* — stub; the fullest treatment is in `../the_dry_boatmen.md`.
> See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** family of standing — the last boat-owning house on Maren's Green
- **Heartland:** Reed Ward; the **Alder Moorings**, a dry warehouse court
- **Trades:** boatwork on the Serle beyond the south wall
- **Anchors:** Wyn Alder (34, best boatwoman on the river); Ewart Alder (49, wants
  to sell); Tobin Alder (17); Rohese of the Sluice (61, keeps the dry gatehouse)
- **Temper:** proud, cash-poor, land-rich, quietly coming apart

When the Serle was moved outside the wall the other boat-families scattered into
safer trades; the Alders kept the name, the yard, and the memory, and are slowly
being ruined by all three. Four Alders hold four different answers to *do we sell
the Moorings* and none is said aloud at the same table. Their one asset that
cannot be repossessed is **the soundings** — the remembered map of where the
river used to run, worth quiet money to masons and well-diggers, and taught by
walking a seven-year-old up the dry Cut. Some of the cell's boat-kin are Alders
by blood. *"Ask an Alder where the bed was."*
