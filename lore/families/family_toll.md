# The Tolls

> *Ironic name, hard edge, wall-quarter house.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Wallwright and Bell-and-Sluice streets
- **Trades:** masonry, pottery, animal-work, baking, service, watch
- **Anchor:** Colm Toll (protection and intimidation)

For a family named for a levy, the Tolls do their own collecting. They fill the
building and service trades of the wall quarter — masons, a potter, a baker,
beast-handlers, a watchman — around Coswald's Yard and the central streets, and
one Toll runs the same kind of neighbourhood shakedown a Skep does at the other
end of the city. A solid wall-quarter house with a hard man in it; reach for a
Toll when the masons' quarter needs muscle, or a name that lands with a small
grim joke.
