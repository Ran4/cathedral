# The Pikes

<img src="crests/family_pike_crest.png" alt="Arms of The Pikes" width="150" align="right">


> *The keys of the cathedral.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** family of standing — cathedral servants
- **Heartland:** Fabric Ward (**the Lanthorn**), branched into Bell-and-Sluice
- **Trades:** vergers and church attendants; also laundry, masonry, pottery, watch
- **Anchors:** Dunstan Pike (49, verger — every door in the Lanthorn answers to
  the keys at his belt); Gile Pike (a paid moth of the Custody); Clemence Pike
  (44, keeps a licensed pilgrim lodging off the Gradine and is one of the
  Fabric Ward's two benchers — a woman of the cathedral's servant-family who
  quarrels with the Chapter anyway; see `../core_lore/ward_politics.md`)

The Pikes keep the doors. Dunstan unbars the west doors for the dawn-showing,
walks the nave and aisles all day, moves pilgrims along, chases out dogs and
dice-players, takes a penny for a good place at the strong hour, and locks up at
dusk — then crosses to Renna Tapster's Bell and Ladle, where his tab is longer
than he admits. A verger with keys is a verger who can be paid to turn one: it is
a Pike who quietly unbars a side door so the physician Ferrant can set his
instruments under the Great Rose, and a Pike who carries faces to the grey coats.
Small, pious, useful, and porous — the cathedral's own servant-family.
