# The Thorns

> *Tanners and porters of the weigh-streets.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Cloth and Weigh streets
- **Trades:** leather-work, salt-work, garment-work, revenue-work, boat-work
- **Anchor:** Ewart Thorn (filing false manifests)

The Thorns work hide, salt and cloth along the weighing and Cloth streets — a
leather-worker, a couple of garment-hands, a salt-worker, a revenue clerk, a
boatman — the middling labour of the trade quarters. The revenue foot is where
the family bends: a Thorn files false manifests, understating a load for the man
who brought it. A serviceable house for tannery, portering and the small
paperwork frauds of the customs edge.
