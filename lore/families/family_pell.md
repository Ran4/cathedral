# The Pells

> *A three-ward family of small gates and poaching.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Cloth, Bell-and-Sluice and Reed streets, in even measure
- **Trades:** cooperage, animal-work, farming, tavern, cooking, baking, fine metal
- **Anchors:** Betriss Pell (gate bribery); Clemence Pell (poaching); Idonea Pell
  (unlicensed vending)

The Pells are a smallholding-and-victualling house spread evenly across three
wards — coopers, beast-handlers, a farmer, a tavern-hand, a baker — the sort who
keep animals, keep a stall, and keep just outside the letter of the licence.
Their little crimes are all of a piece: a coin to a gate-man, a hare taken where
one shouldn't be, a board set up without paying the vendor's due. A broad,
low-standing, useful family for populating a market row or a gate, with a
reliable readiness to look the other way for a spark.
