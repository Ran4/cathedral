# The Netts

> *A quiet cloth-and-carpentry house.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Cloth and Weigh streets, evenly split
- **Trades:** garment-work, cooperage, cargo, grocer-spicer, carpentry
- **Anchors:** none of note

The Netts are a small, steady house of the cloth and weighing streets — a
garment-hand, a cooper, a couple of carpenters, a grocer-spicer, a cargo-hand —
makers and movers of modest standing, with nothing crooked or dramatic in the
record. One of the plainer names in the roster; cast a Nett when a scene needs an
honest joiner, a seamstress, or a spice-stall without a story attached.
