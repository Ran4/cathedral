# The Husks

> *Drovers of the Tallage edge.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Weigh Ward streets
- **Trades:** animal-work, cartwrighting, fish-trading, service, market, labour
- **Anchors:** none of note

The Husks are a droving-and-carting house of the weighing quarter — beast-handlers,
a cartwright, a fish-trader, a stallholder — the muscle and wheels that keep the
Tallage fed and moving. Low-standing, unremarkable, uncrooked in the record: a
plain working name for the men who bring the beasts to market and haul what the
weighing quarter buys.
