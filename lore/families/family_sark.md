# The Sarks

> *Washtub and cook-pot.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Reed, Cloth and Bell-and-Sluice streets
- **Trades:** laundry, animal-work, cooking, baking, scribing, watch, fine metal
- **Anchors:** none of note

A *sark* is a shirt, and the Sarks keep the city's linen and its cook-pots — a
couple of laundresses, a cook, a baker, with a scribe and a watchman at the
edges — humble domestic-and-kitchen labour spread across the Reed and Cloth
streets. An honest, hard-worn house with no feud and no faction in the record.
Cast a Sark for a washhouse full of gossip, or a kitchen where the ward's
business is overheard between the pots.
