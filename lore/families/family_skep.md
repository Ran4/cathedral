# The Skeps

<img src="crests/family_skep_crest.png" alt="Arms of The Skeps" width="150" align="right">


> *A rougher house — protection and threat.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice, Weigh and Wick streets
- **Trades:** service, cooperage, milling, fish, boat-work, militia
- **Anchor:** Gile Skep (protection and intimidation)

A *skep* is a straw beehive or carrying-basket — a humble thing — but the Skeps
have a harder side than most ambient houses. They fill the ordinary poor trades
of the central and weighing streets (servants, coopers, a miller, fish-hands,
soldiers), and one Skep runs a protection racket, leaning on stallholders and
small men for a steady cut. Reach for a Skep when a scene needs muscle that
belongs to the neighbourhood rather than to any faction — the sort who is owed a
little by everyone on the street and collects without raising his voice.
