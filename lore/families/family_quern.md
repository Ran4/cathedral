# The Querns

> *The light-fingered milling-poor.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house — poor, un-propertied
- **Heartland:** Wick and Bell-and-Sluice streets
- **Trades:** baking, service, shoemaking, food-selling — and a great deal of small crime
- **Anchors:** Corin Quern (unregistered brothel); Jos Quern (receiving stolen
  goods); Renna Quern (burglary); Gude Quern (pickpocketing)

A *quern* is a poor household's hand-mill, and the Querns are exactly that
class — the working poor of the crowded Wick and central streets, in service,
behind bread-boards, at cobbler's lasts and food-stalls. More than any other
name, the roster shows their poverty as small crime: an unlicensed brothel,
fenced household goods, a burglary, a picked pocket. None of it is grand; all of
it is the economy of the poor quarters wearing the family's name. A useful house
to reach for when a scene needs a receiver, a lookout, or a hand that has been
somewhere it should not.
