# The Copps

> *A tiny, dangerous house of two prices.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md)
> and `../second_sun/02_the_heretic_cell.md` (the Ledger Theft).

- **Standing:** family of standing — small, pivotal, and crooked
- **Heartland:** Weigh Ward; the **Tallage**, a pawnshop facing the **Tally Bridge**
- **Trades:** clerking and forgery; pawnbroking and fencing; paper
- **Anchors:** Corin Copp (26, best hand at the Tallage — forger of old pages by
  candle); Lise Copp (55, his aunt — pawnbroker and fence)

Two Copps, and between them they turn the city's paper. Corin writes honest bills
of lading, toll receipts and ledgers by day and, by candle, makes *old* pages:
oak-gall browned over smoke, a seal aged in a flame, a dead man's hesitation
copied into every fourth letter. They go out across Lise's counter — dawn to
dusk, unhurried, unreadable, everything at two prices and no questions about
provenance included in the rate. It is over that counter that the stolen archive
pages of the Ledger Theft move: to the Grey Press one week and the Unwalled the
next, the cell buying page by page toward the sealed Sparr deposition. A house of
two people who quietly decide what the written record of Ombreval says.
