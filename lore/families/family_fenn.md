# The Fenns

> *A marsh-name house, half in the watch.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice streets
- **Trades:** militia and soldiering, chandlery, shoemaking, sanitation, food
- **Anchors:** none of note

A *fenn* is a marsh, and the Fenns are a plain central-ward house with a strong
foot in the militia — a couple of soldiers, a chandler, a shoemaker, a
sanitation-hand, food-sellers — the working population of the Bell-and-Sluice
streets. Uncrooked in the record and unremarkable by design; a good default for a
gate-guard, a candle-seller, or a street-sweeper who has seen who comes and goes
by the sluice.
