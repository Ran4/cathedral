# The Dunns

> *A small, humble house.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house — one of the smallest named in the roster
- **Heartland:** Weigh and Bell-and-Sluice streets
- **Trades:** rope-making, tavern, animal-work, sanitation
- **Anchors:** none of note

The Dunns are a handful of working folk in the weighing and central streets — a
roper, a tavern-hand, a beast-handler, a sanitation-worker — barely a house at
all, and the sort of name that a generation of bad luck could tip out of standing
and into bynames. Nothing crooked, nothing grand; a small honest presence, and a
reminder that most named families are only a few members and one hard winter from
the roster's bottom edge.
