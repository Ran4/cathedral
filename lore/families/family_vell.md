# The Vells

> *Risen chandlers with a heretic at the head.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md),
> `../second_sun/02_the_heretic_cell.md`, and `../the_dry_boatmen.md` (the fallen
> Reed-Ward Vells).

- **Standing:** family of standing — but split between a risen branch and a fallen one
- **Heartland:** Wick Ward (the **Wickmarket**); a poorer Reed-Ward branch
- **Trades:** chandlery, wax, lamplighting, church attendance — the trades of light
- **Anchor:** Osanne Vell (63), mistress of the Vell wax-house, and secretly the
  **Tracer** — hidden head of the Unwalled

The Vell name has two ends. On the Wickmarket, Osanne runs the wax-house and has
held the Lanthorn's candle contract in her own name for thirty years, so no
verger looks twice when a Vell basket goes into the cathedral — perfect cover for
the one person who holds the whole tracery of the cell in her head: the lights,
the leads, the pass rule, the sign rotation. Her son Ewart runs the floor and
keeps the books, believing he runs the family. At the other end, on Maren's
Green, are the fallen Vells — *the Vells had a warehouse once*, and now sell
pilgrim badges in the shadow of somebody else's. Wax, tallow, wick and flame run
right through the name, which is the family's private joke and its deepest cover.
The Vells are the watched to the [Rasps'](family_rasp.md) watcher.
