# The Cloves

> *Sweet name, hard hands; drovers and debt-men.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice and Weigh streets
- **Trades:** droving and animal-work, cargo, brewing, militia, civic office, service
- **Anchors:** Ede Clove (violent debt collection); Betriss Clove (taking bribes
  during inspection)

The name says spice; the hands say otherwise. The Cloves handle beasts and loads
in the central and weighing streets — drovers, a carter, a brewer, a soldier, a
minor inspector — muscle-and-movement work, and it shows in their record: a debt
collected with fists, an inspection sold for a bribe. A useful house for a scene
that needs weight leaned on someone, or an official whose stamp is for sale, all
under a name that sounds like a kitchen shelf.
