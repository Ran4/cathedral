# The Larks

> *A carting-and-cook-pot house.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Weigh and Bell-and-Sluice streets
- **Trades:** cooperage, cartwrighting, tavern, cooking, carpentry, scavenging,
  court office
- **Anchors:** none of note

The Larks move things and feed people along the weighing and central streets — a
cooper, a cartwright, a tavern-hand, a cook, a carpenter — with one member risen
into a court office. A middling, mobile, well-connected house of no faction and
no notable crime, at home wherever carts are loaded and pots are stirred. Cast a
Lark for a busy carter, a tap-room cook, or a minor court usher who knows the
whole street.
