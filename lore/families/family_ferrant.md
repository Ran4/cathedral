# The Ferrants

> *A learned singular name under the Rose.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** family of standing — learned, and (so far) a house of one
- **Heartland:** Weigh Ward; a tall narrow house off the **Tallage**
- **Trades:** physic and astronomy
- **Anchor:** Aubin Ferrant (52), physician by trade and astronomer by affliction

The Ferrant name carries one man and a great deal of quiet danger. Aubin keeps a
tall house off the Tallage: sick-calls all morning through fevers, crushed
wharf-hands and hard births (the midwife Petronel Skell sends for him to save the
ones she cannot), and then the nave at the strong hour, where a Pike verger
unbars a side door for a coin and lets him set his instruments up under the Great
Rose. He is courteous to every soul and studies the very thing the Custody exists
to keep unstudied — the light through the glass — which makes a mild physician one
of the most quietly seditious men in the city without a heretic thought in his
head. Whether there are other Ferrants to come is open.
