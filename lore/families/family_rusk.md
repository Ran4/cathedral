# The Rusks

> *The victualler-and-hustle house.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice and Reed streets, with a Cloth branch
- **Trades:** tavern-keeping, baking, cooking, brewing, a merchant, a guide, service
- **Anchors:** Aubin Rusk (running illegal gambling); Corin Rusk (selling
  counterfeit pilgrim badges)

A *rusk* is twice-baked bread, and the Rusks are a food-and-drink family who have
learned to sell more than bread. They keep tap-rooms, ovens, cook-fires and a
brewhouse in the central and Reed streets, and off the back of the pilgrim traffic
they run the small hustles the trade allows: a back-room dice game, a counterfeit
pilgrim badge that looks near enough by candlelight. Warm, sociable, always where
the crowd and the coin are — the family to reach for when a scene needs a busy
tavern, a tout, or a badge that will not survive daylight.
