# The Dorns

- **Standing:** family of standing — but here the name *is* the office
- **Heartland:** the **Lanthorn** and the chapter close, Fabric Ward
- **Trades:** the Church; the highest cloth in Ombreval
- **Anchor:** Havise Dorn (58), **Praelucent of Ombreval**, lamp-named *Serene*

The Dorn name carries a single towering figure and little else in the roster —
Havise Dorn, head of the Church, whose day is the dawn office, chapter-house
audiences until High Wick, and the crossing at the strong hour, hearing petitions
and Fabric disputes and the endless snarl of masons and glaziers, and doling out
kindness the way a careful house doles out coin. She speaks slowly and exactly
and gives no ruling she can withhold. Whether *Dorn* is a lineage of standing or
a name that rose with one woman into the Praelucency is a thing to decide later;
for now it is the closest the families come to a crown.
