# The Hobbes

<img src="crests/family_hobbe_crest.png" alt="Arms of The Hobbes" width="150" align="right">


> *Went to the cart.* — stub. See `../the_dry_boatmen.md` and [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** family of standing — boat-blood turned land-carriers
- **Heartland:** Fabric and Bell-and-Sluice streets (dispersed from the Reed Ward)
- **Trades:** carting, freight, milling, bell-ringing, building, clergy
- **Anchors:** Renn Hobbe (34, wick-priest of Saint Maren of the Reeds — a
  boat-family son in the boat-family's pulpit); Gile Hobbe (a paid moth of the
  Custody, a word he will not use); Segwin Hobbe (56, carter to the Fabric
  works and one of the Fabric Ward's two benchers — the ward's tax quarrel
  with the Chapter rides on his own wagon; see
  `../core_lore/ward_politics.md`)

The Hobbes made peace with the enemy: they carry wool and freight up from the
gate, which means they eat off the **dry carry** — the doubled handling every
other boat-family curses. They are boat-family by blood and, in the strictest
ward politics, the people who profit from the wound; at a funeral everyone stands
in the same churchyard and nobody says it. Renn preaches to men who think his
brothers robbed them, and is very careful which parable he chooses. The name has
travelled well up into the Fabric and central streets — into mills, belfries,
building yards and one grey-coat's quiet ledger.
