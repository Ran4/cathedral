# The Sparrs

> *Idonea's line — the watched glass.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md) and
> `../second_sun/02_the_heretic_cell.md` (the Glazier Rule).

- **Standing:** family of standing — master glaziers
- **Heartland:** **Cinder Row** (the glass furnaces), Cinder Ward
- **Trades:** glazing, leading, furnace-work for the Fabric
- **Anchors:** Jonet Sparr (44, master glazier, of Idonea's line)

The Sparrs hold the Row's first furnace and descend from **Idonea Sparr**, whom
the heretics quietly venerate — which is exactly why no Sparr may be sworn to the
cell (the **Glazier Rule**: one glazier taken at a meeting and F.288 comes back
for all Cinder Row). Every repair pane they make for the cathedral is cut under
the eye of a grey clerk who writes down how many times they touch it. They are
wry, guild-proud and long-memoried; they swear apprentices on the lead, not the
glass. Somewhere sealed in the record is the **Sparr deposition** the Unwalled
are buying toward, page by page, across Lise Copp's counter — the thing the whole
cell is patiently reaching for. They are also the family the Cinder Row Crake has
an old, unspoken deed-claim against.
