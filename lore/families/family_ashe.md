# The Ashes

> *The salt house whose cellar hides the grate.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md)
> and `../second_sun/02_the_heretic_cell.md`.

- **Standing:** family of standing — salt
- **Heartland:** Weigh Ward (the **Tallage**, **Gaunt Passage**) and the Reed streets
- **Trades:** salt-trading and salt-work; also laundry, pottery, entertaining, watch
- **Anchor:** Grigor Ashe (41, salt-merchant; the **Wicket** — doorkeeper of the
  Unwalled; keeps a private list of faces)

Salt is the lawful Ashe trade — Salorge salt poled upriver, carted through the
gate, weighed at Lowmarket, stored in the cellars by the Tallage — and Grigor can
tell to the grain what a salt-weigh ought to come to, so nobody cheats him twice.
Under one of those cellars, off Gaunt Passage, is the grate; on meeting nights
Grigor is the door — a quarter-hour, no longer, the name whispered and the sign
returned. He keeps the door because the door is his floor: jumpy, mercenary, a
quarter-believer who is in it as much for the cellar as the sun. The broader Ashe
name is a working salt-and-laundry house scattered from Cinder to Reed to Weigh,
none of them knowing whose cellar the family's most careful son keeps.
