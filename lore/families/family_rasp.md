# The Rasps

> *One grey eminence over a mass of poor.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md)
> and `../second_sun/05_dramatis_personae.md`.

- **Standing:** family of standing — by virtue of one office, not one workshop
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice and Wick streets; the head sits in the **Grey
  Press** (Fabric)
- **Trades:** cooperage, scavenging, brewing, cartage, pilgrim-service — and the
  Custody of the Eye
- **Anchor:** Segwin Rasp (54), **Provost-Custodian**, master of the Custody of
  the Eye, keeper of the Grey Press

The Rasps are the watching family, but only at the top. Segwin runs the Grey
Press bell to bell — licensing glass, ordering the pilgrim hours, stamping
badges, filing every claim made about the Emblem and quietly disproving most;
dry, meticulous, he corrects rather than punishes and wants written *what a man
saw, not what he thought it meant*. Below him the name is entirely ordinary: a
long tail of coopers, scavengers, brewers, pilgrims and labourers in the poor
central and Wick streets, who mostly have no idea a Rasp keeps the city's file of
faces. The family is the whole quiet war in one surname — a grey coat at the top
and the watched poor at the bottom. Its natural opposite is the [Vells](family_vell.md).
