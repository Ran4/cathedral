# The Kerns

<img src="crests/family_kern_crest.png" alt="Arms of The Kerns" width="150" align="right">


> *The most literate — and shabbiest — of the ambient houses.* — stub. See
> [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house — but a lettered one
- **Heartland:** Cloth and Weigh streets
- **Trades:** scribing, scholarship, drapery, merchanting, carpentry, salt, scavenging
- **Anchors:** Renna Kern (coin clipping); Bertran Kern (toll evasion); Osanne
  Kern (receiving stolen goods)

Alone among the ambient houses the Kerns keep a pen and a book: a scribe, a
scholar, a draper and a merchant sit in the name beside the labourers and the
scavenger. That little learning cuts both ways — the family's crimes are the
clever kind (a clipped coin, a dodged toll, a fenced parcel) rather than the
brutal kind. A faintly shabby-genteel house, the sort with more letters than
coin, useful when a scene wants a clerk who reads too much, a scholar down on his
luck, or a draper whose books do not quite add up.
