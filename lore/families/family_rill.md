# The Rills

> *A little water-name house.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice streets
- **Trades:** rope-making, animal-work, entertaining, food, service
- **Anchor:** Idonea Rill (receiving stolen goods)

A *rill* is a small stream — a wry name in a city whose river was walled out — and
the Rills are a small central-ward house to match: a roper, a beast-handler, an
entertainer, a food-seller, a servant. One Rill quietly receives stolen goods,
the neighbourhood's small back-door trade. A minor, low-standing name; useful for
a rope-walk, a street act, or a household that will hold a thing for you and ask
nothing.
