# The Brants

> *The city hears a Brant before its own bells.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** family of standing — the public voice of the squares
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice (the **Bellstand**), branched into Cinder and Fabric
- **Trades:** crying and messaging; healing, masonry, smithing, painting, militia
- **Anchors:** Jos Brant (44, crier of the Bellstand — an *unwitting* paid moth of
  the Custody)

The Brants carry the city's word. Jos walks the circuit of the five squares at
set hours crying edicts, prices, strays, sales and hangings, word for word as
they are handed him, and can still recite every proclamation of twenty years —
loud, warm, incurably cheerful, and known to a thousand people by name. He is fed
stew at Renna Tapster's Bell and Ladle for the news he carries in, and his
cheerful mouth is being used: he is a paid moth of the Custody who does not know
it, the man who hears everything and repeats it to one grey coat too many. The
wider Brant name spreads into healing, masonry, smithing and the militia across
the central and Cinder streets — a family whose gift and curse is that it cannot
help talking.
