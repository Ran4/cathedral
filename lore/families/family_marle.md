# The Marles

> *A cloth-and-cathedral house with a heretic heart.* — stub. See
> [overview](overview.md) and `../second_sun/02_the_heretic_cell.md`.

- **Standing:** family of standing — cloth and building trades
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice and Fabric streets; branched into Reed
- **Trades:** cloth-work, masonry, cargo, civic office, militia, market — with a
  strong daily presence in the nave
- **Anchor:** Betriss Marle (38, seamstress; publicly a recanted heretic of F.428;
  secretly the **Namekeeper** of the Unwalled)

The Marles work cloth and stone and turn up daily in the cathedral, which is how
one of them became the cell's best-placed eye. Betriss recanted on the Gradine in
F.428 — wept as the crowd required — and has been the woman neighbours spit near
ever since; the recantation was theatre, planned with the cell to shield the
Tracer, whose name she alone keeps. She sews all day near the crossing where the
whole nave can see her being devout, keeps the **bede-roll** of the cell's dead
in memory (never ink), and charts the sky in embroidery. The wider Marle name
runs to masons, criers, soldiers and a few hard cases (false manifests, violent
debt-collection, protection) across the central and Fabric streets — an ordinary,
respectable-enough house carrying one secret keeper of names.
