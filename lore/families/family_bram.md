# The Brams

> *A soldiering family with a cook's sideline.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Wick and Bell-and-Sluice streets
- **Trades:** militia and soldiering (heavily), milling, healing, food, fine metal
- **Anchor:** Clemence Bram (adulterating food)

The Brams lean to the militia more than any other ambient house — several sons in
the watch and the town soldiery — with a miller, a healer and a couple of
food-sellers rounding out the name across the Wick and central streets. The
food-foot has the family's one blemish: a Bram who stretches the meat with
something cheaper. A good house for guards, gate-soldiers and the men of the
watch, with a cook-pot and a mill to send them home to.
