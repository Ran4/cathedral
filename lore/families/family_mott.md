- **Standing:** family of standing — mixed, with one clerk in the Custody
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice streets, branched into Fabric
- **Trades:** butchery, bellfounding, entertaining, gaoling, garment-work — and
  a Custody clerkship
- **Anchors:** Sible Mott (27, clerk of the Custody of the Eye, works the Grey
  Press); Noll Mott (illicit slaughter)

The Motts are an ordinary central-ward house — a butcher, a bellfounder, a couple
of entertainers, a gaoler — with one member inside the grey walls. Sible spends
her days in the Grey Press making other men's bad handwriting legible, and chief
among her copy-work are the fair copies of Clerk Tilman Rue's **funeral
watch-lists**: faces, not names, the same faces lingering at Saint Maren's
charnel door burial after burial. She is a Mott who has read, in a good clean
hand, exactly who keeps turning up to count a name-knell — which makes this small
family quietly one of the most dangerous in the city, and Sible one of the few
who could ruin the Unwalled with a page.
