# The Stotts

> *The lodge and the plumb-line grudge.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** family of standing — masons
- **Heartland:** **Wallwright Ward**; the masons' lodge at **Coswald's Yard** and
  the north tower scaffold
- **Trades:** masonry and stone-contracting to the Fabric; branched into watch,
  grocering, messengers, market
- **Anchors:** Hamel Stott (47, Warden of the masons' lodge, contractor to the
  Fabric); some Stotts on gate-bribes and inspection-bribes

The Stotts are stone. Hamel keeps forty men in work and bread, argues rates with
the Fabric's clerks, and holds a grudge as straight as a plumb-line; he ends
arguments the way a wall ends an argument. Their heart is up the scaffold and in
the lodge, in the endless, arguably-permanent construction of the cathedral —
where mason and glazier and carpenter snarl at each other over the same
contracts. The name has spilled out of Wallwright into the central and Wick
streets as watchmen, messengers and stallholders, some of whom have learned that
a man who keeps a gate can be paid to look the other way.
