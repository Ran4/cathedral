# The Skells

<img src="crests/family_skell_crest.png" alt="Arms of The Skells" width="150" align="right">


> *Let the boat go.* — stub. See `../the_dry_boatmen.md` and [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** family of standing — numerically the largest house in the roster
- **Heartland:** Reed Ward (Maren's Green), now branched into half the city
- **Trades:** fish, eel, smoke-racks, wharf wages, burial, drapery, healing, watch
- **Anchors:** Averil Skell (52, fish-smoker, the practical power of the Green,
  lends at a spark on the bell); Petronel Skell (midwife, sent for at hard
  births); Gile Skell (thief)

The Skells got off the water and took the trades that stayed *inside* the wall,
and it was the winning answer: *"An Alder owns a boat and owes for it. A Skell
owns nothing and is never hungry."* Where a Skell still crews, he crews for a
wage on someone else's hull and goes home at the gate without a care for what it
cost. Three generations later the name has spread further than any other — into
drapers, healers, watchmen, potters and painters across the Reed, Cloth, Weigh
and Wallwright streets — a broad, comfortable, undramatic house that owns the
one thing the diversion could not take: not being hungry.
