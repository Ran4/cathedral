# The Ruds

> *The angry cloth-poor; the raw timber of the Unwalled.* — stub. See
> [overview](overview.md) and `../second_sun/02_the_heretic_cell.md`.

- **Standing:** ambient house — cloth-poor, no property to hold a name proudly
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice, Wick and Cinder streets; the fulling stocks on
  the Cut
- **Trades:** fulling and cloth-work, soldiering, messengers, service, sanitation
- **Anchors:** Tam Rud (29, journeyman fuller; sworn Unwalled of the **Breach
  faction**); Gile Rud (warehouse sabotage)

The Ruds are the poor cloth-workers whose day is stale piss, hot lye and wet wool
in the fulling stocks, for three bells a day and a master who counts every ell.
Tam is angry the way wet wool is heavy — all through, and slow to dry — and he is
the post-Shut-Door intake, the six sworn *against the Church* rather than *for the
sun*, whose seam shows in the cell. The Breach faction he leans to whispers that
the *building itself* is the wall and talks of opening it: all talk, no
capability, but talk that could hang all nineteen. Where the Vells and Marles are
the cell's patient old head, the Ruds are its raw, dangerous new muscle — and a
Rud has already put his anger into a warehouse.
