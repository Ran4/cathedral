# The Sedges

> *The weighing-quarter's small-fraud family.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Weigh and Cloth streets
- **Trades:** service, grocer-spicer, money-dealing, tavern, salt, court office, market
- **Anchors:** Osanne Sedge (coin clipping); Corin Sedge (watering ale);
  Rohese Sedge (58, grocer and one of the Weigh Ward's two benchers —
  scrupulously, conspicuously exact, one true measure at a time, which is the
  family's rehabilitation and everyone knows it; see
  `../core_lore/ward_politics.md`)

The Sedges live off the counter and the measure in the weighing quarter — a
grocer-spicer, a small money-dealer, a taverner, a court usher, stall-keepers —
the middling working folk of the Tallage's edge. Their besetting sin is the one
the trade invites: shaving the coin and watering the ale, a spark skimmed here
and a measure short there. Not a dangerous house, but a slippery one: the family
you cast when a scene turns on a false weight, a clipped penny, or a jug that
does not taste like it should.
