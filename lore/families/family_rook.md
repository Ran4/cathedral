# The Rooks

> *A servant-and-belfry house round the towers.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Bell-and-Sluice and Weigh streets
- **Trades:** domestic service (heavily), bell-ringing, cooperage, cobbling,
  entertaining, labour
- **Anchors:** none of note

The Rooks are a house of servants and one bell-ringer, clustered like their
namesakes around the central towers and the weighing streets — in other people's
kitchens, at the ropes of the belfry, at a cobbler's last, on a labouring gang.
Low-standing, numerous, uncrooked in the record, they are the domestic
population of the middle city: the family whose members are *in* the great
houses and the great church without belonging to either. Cast a Rook for a maid, a
ringer, or a hired pair of hands who sees everything and is never asked.
