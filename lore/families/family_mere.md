# The Meres

> *Victuallers with a false-manifest sideline.* — stub. See [overview](overview.md).

- **Standing:** ambient house
- **Heartland:** Cloth and Bell-and-Sluice streets
- **Trades:** cooperage, cooking, baking, tavern, scribing
- **Anchors:** Dunstan Mere (filing false manifests); Jos Mere (39, victualler
  and carrier, one of the Cloth Ward's two benchers — the ward returns him
  because a man who dislikes scrutiny of carts fights cart fees with perfect
  sincerity; see `../core_lore/ward_politics.md`)

The Meres are a small food-and-cask house of the Cloth and central streets — a
cooper, a cook, a baker, a tavern-hand — with a scribe who has learned to make a
manifest say what the payer wants. A modest victualling family whose one crooked
member sits, tellingly, at the pen rather than the pot: the Meres cheat on paper,
not in the jug. Useful for a barrel-yard, a cook-shop, or a load whose papers do
not match its weight.
