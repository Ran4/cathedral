# The Impossible Light

> Fixed observable canon for the appearance in the Great Rose. Use only when
> the light, the Lanthorn, related belief, or conflicting explanations matter.

## The impossible light

The light is part of shared world canon even when a lore dump is about
something else. Its **observable rules** are fixed:

1. A second sunlike disc is visible only in sky seen **through the glass of the
   Great Rose, from inside the Lanthorn**. Other windows show nothing. Outside
   the building there is one sun.
2. A missing pane's open gap shows an ordinary sky. A pane removed from the
   Rose and carried elsewhere shows nothing. New plain glass leaded into the
   Rose shows the disc perfectly. The effect belongs to the Rose in place, not
   to a special piece of old glass.
3. The disc is cold green-white, often compared to dawn seen through river ice.
   It keeps its own colour through every coloured pane. It can be looked at
   directly without pain.
4. It casts visible green-white beams but no heat. Wax does not soften in them.
   It heals nothing, speaks no words, and leaves no physical trace.
5. Within its light, people and objects cast a faint second **grave-shadow** at
   an angle inconsistent with the true sun. The two kinds of light overlap
   without visually mixing.
6. It occupies the same pane for every viewer in the building at a given
   moment, despite their different positions, yet its rays move across the
   floor with the hour. This lack of parallax has no accepted explanation.
7. It moves against the true sun's course: the **countergait**. It is low and
   large in the western Rose at the **dawn-showing**, shares the window with
   the true sun during the late-afternoon **strong hour**, and crosses it once
   daily in **the Passing**, called **the Kiss** in street speech.
8. Once a year, on **the Concurrence**, the Passing centres in the eye from the
   crossing floor and doubled shadows temporarily fuse. If cloud hides the
   true sun, the pale disc remains visible but there is no fusion; the feast
   simply fails.
9. It remains visible through daytime overcast. During lightning every pane may
   flash white except the place where the disc stands, which does not brighten.
10. It never appears in the Serle, rain barrels, or mirrors carried outdoors.
    No moon, star, flame, or other heavenly body doubles.

The Candor calls it **the Emblem**, a granted image of the one Light:
***Una lux, gemina species***, “one light, twofold appearance.” The street calls
it **the Green Sun** despite the Edict. Folk say the cold light belongs to the
dead, speak dead names into its beam, hold newborns in it for a “spare shadow,”
and avoid a grave-shadow on a bride. These practices are sincere; their claimed
effects are never confirmed or mocked by authorial voice.

No document, character, system, or future lore dump should reveal the light's
origin, intention, or ultimate nature. It does not choose, answer, escalate, or
perform. Its force comes from being identical every day in a world otherwise
governed by ordinary causes.
