# Population of Ombreval

> Core population canon.

## Before the Great Rains and the Hammering

Ombreval was built for, and once held, approximately **15,000 people** within
its walls. Its dense housing, markets, workshops, warehouses, churches, and
public works belong to a city of that size.

## The present city

By the present year, **F.437**, only about **5,000 people** live in Ombreval.
The decline began when the Great Rains of F.362 broke the Cut and displaced
the old waterside economy. The Great Hail of F.415—the Hammering—then killed
thousands, and approximately 3,000–4,000 survivors left during the Long
Departure.

The figures are estimates rather than a complete census. See
[`lore/the_great_rains_and_the_hammering.md`](../the_great_rains_and_the_hammering.md)
for the trajectory, its uncertainties, and the distinction between the three
names.

The city therefore feels a little too large for its inhabitants. Many buildings
and upper floors stand empty or are used only for storage. Some courts and lanes
remain lively because the surviving population gathers around work, worship,
water, and markets, while other streets are conspicuously quiet. Ombreval is
still a functioning city, not an abandoned ruin, but it lives inside the shell
of its more populous past.

## Game representation

[The game represents only a few hundred individual characters—currently around
**500**—for practical and gameplay reasons. These characters are a selective
simulation of the population, not the whole of it. In-world, approximately
4,500 additional people live in Ombreval; people absent from the simulated
cast continue to exist as ordinary residents.]
